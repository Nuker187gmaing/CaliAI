SPECIAL BUREAU OPERATIONS (SBO)
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
================================================================================

SECTION 1 | INTRODUCTION
================================================================================
This document covers all regulations and structure of the SBO department,
encompassing everything required to conduct daily operations. It is MANDATORY
to abide by this SOP — all personnel will be held accountable. Failure to do so
will result in punishment or discharge.

For questions regarding listed information, contact a member of High Command.

SECTION 2 | DISCORD REGULATIONS
================================================================================
All members of the SBO Agency Discord are expected to maintain professionalism
and respect at all times.

- Toxic behavior, harassment, discrimination, or unnecessary drama will NOT be
  tolerated under any circumstances.
- Members must follow all server rules and use channels for their intended purposes.
- No spam or misuse of communication systems.

- All members must respect staff decisions; keep disagreements civil and handled

Failure to follow these guidelines may result in warnings, removal of roles, or
permanent removal from the Discord and agency.

RTO ETIQUETTE AND 10 CODES
================================================================================

BASIC RADIO ETIQUETTE:
- Mandatory operational language in radio channels: English.
- Do NOT interrupt unless your traffic is pertinent to a serious emergency.
- Do NOT respond if you aren't sure the call is for you; wait for your callsign.
- Never transmit sensitive, confidential, financial, or military information.
- Memorize callsigns and locations of persons/radio stations you communicate with.
- Avoid long, complicated sentences; divide long messages into shorter ones.
- Transmissions must be professional at all times (they are recorded and monitored).
- Keep communications clear, simple, and short. Use 10-codes as much as possible.
- Callsign and Name must be displayed properly and match department credentials.

DISPATCHING POLICY
================================================================================
WITH DISPATCH ONLINE:
- Take all commands from Dispatch.
- If dispatched to a scene, notify them you are available and en-route.
- For a 10-11 Traffic Stop: notify Dispatch first, then WAIT for them to tell you
  to proceed with further information. DO NOT ARGUE WITH DISPATCH.
- If Dispatch does not request a certain response, you may inform them you are
  en route and responding Code 1, 2, or 3 as appropriate.

SELF DISPATCHING (no Dispatch online):
- Units call out situations over the radio and may request backup as needed.
- You are permitted to self-dispatch yourself to a request for additional units.

SECTION 3 | IN-GAME REGULATIONS
================================================================================
All SBO Agents must maintain the highest level of professionalism while in-game.

- Follow all server rules, agency SOPs, and lawful procedures during every
  interaction.
- Abuse of power, unrealistic roleplay, excessive force without justification, or
  failure to follow proper protocols will NOT be tolerated.
- Treat all civilians, suspects, and fellow members with respect and fairness.
- Remain in character while on-duty; avoid behavior that disrupts immersion or
  damages the integrity of the roleplay environment.

Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action including suspension, demotion,
or termination.

MEMBER QUALIFICATION:
To join SBO, applicants must:
- Be registered in the CaliRP CAD System.
- Have a clean criminal record.
- Have a valid driver's license.
- Possess the ability to qualify for a firearms permit and license.
- Possess the skills necessary to perform department duties.

IDENTIFICATION POLICY:
- You will be assigned a callsign upon joining.
- You are identified by your callsign and CAD-registered name while on-duty.
  Example formats: XXXX | J. Smith  /  John S.  /  John Smith
- Your name and callsign MUST be in your Steam Profile Name or FiveM Display Name
  so you can be identified by server staff while on-duty.

================================================================================

================================================================================
ON-SCENE PROTOCOL
================================================================================
PRIORITY SCENES:
- You are always permitted to respond to priority scenes.
- You may NEVER FORCE scene command — you MAY REQUEST to take scene command.
- This is especially important on scenes where scene command isn't established.

NON-PRIORITY SCENES:
- You are always permitted to respond to non-priority scenes (10-10, 10-11,
  10-17, 10-18, etc.).
- You are still NEVER allowed to force scene command.

HOMEFRONT SECURITY (DHS)
================================================================================
DHS ensures adherence to CaliRP Global SOP. DHS Agents may attach to a member's
call or conduct a traffic stop. DHS Agents may participate in general LEO roleplay.

- If questioned by a DHS Agent, answer to the best of your ability and comply
  with any instructions given.
- Minor issues may be corrected at the scene; DHS reserves the right to report
  findings to department High Command.
- For issues/concerns over DHS conduct: use the CaliRP Department Ticket process
  or contact a DHS Supervisor.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RANK DUTIES:

High Command:
- Management and oversight of the department as a whole.

Low Command:
- Fulfill administrative requests.
- Oversee department activities such as training and recruiting.
- Provide oversight and management of Supervisors.

Supervisors:
- Host trainings and recruit new members.
- Oversee Field/Patrol units and ensure SOP adherence.

Field/Patrol Units:
- Patrol and perform the duties assigned to the department.
- Attend and assist with trainings.

================================================================================
PROMOTIONAL GUIDELINES / ACTIVITY REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================
WEEKLY MINIMUM: 5 hours of on-duty activity per week.
- Must consist of active patrol or assigned operations.
- Simply being in-game or AFK does NOT count toward activity.
- Must complete at least 3 patrol sessions per week (minimum 1 hour each).
- Must actively engage in call response, traffic enforcement, and realistic
  roleplay at all times.

MEETINGS & TRAININGS:
- Attendance at all mandatory meetings and trainings is REQUIRED.
- Missing 2 consecutive meetings without prior notice may result in disciplinary
  action including possible removal.

CADETS & PROBATIONARY AGENTS:
- Held to a higher training standard.
- Must complete at least 2 ride-alongs per week.
- Must actively participate and submit required training documentation.

SPECIALIZED DIVISIONS (Tactical, Investigations, etc.):
- Must still meet standard weekly activity hours.
- Must participate in at least 1 unit-specific operation or training per week.

LOA REQUIREMENT:
- If unable to meet activity requirements, submit a Leave of Absence (LOA) with
  a valid reason and expected return date. Failure to do so = marked inactive.

INACTIVITY CONSEQUENCES:
- 7 days inactive without notice: Warning
- 14 days inactive: Removal from the agency

================================================================================
PUNISHMENT GUIDELINES / STRIKE POLICY
================================================================================
RANK AUTHORITY:
- High Command: may administer any punishment to anyone in the department.
- Low Command: may strike and suspend Supervisors and below.
- Supervisors: may strike or send off-duty anyone below them.

STRIKE SYSTEM:
  Strike 1: No additional action unless otherwise dictated.
  Strike 2: 1-3 day suspension and promotion cooldown.
  Strike 3: Extended suspension and demotion OR termination.

Strikes remain on a member's record for 14 days.

ALTERNATIVE ACTIONS:
- Loss of certain duties, authority, or permissions.
- Administering a refresher training.
- Terminations are the SOLE discretion of High Command.
- If an offense warrants it, High Command may authorize an individual to be
  BLACKLISTED from the department.

================================================================================
CODE OF CONDUCT
================================================================================
While on or off duty, all members represent the department. Members are held to
a higher standard and must reflect that in every action taken, regardless of
whether they are on duty or not.

================================================================================
ACTIVITY AND LOA POLICY
================================================================================
WEEKLY REQUIREMENT:
- Minimum 5 hours of on-duty activity per week.
- Hours must consist of active patrol, call response, or assigned operations.
- Must reflect consistent engagement in realistic roleplay.
- Failure to meet the weekly requirement without an approved LOA may result in
  disciplinary action including warnings, rank review, or removal.

LEAVE OF ABSENCE (LOA):
- If expecting to be absent for more than 1 week, submit an LOA request for
  approval including a valid reason and expected return date.
- If an LOA is to exceed 60 days (2 months), High Command reserves the right to
  seek termination if there was no prior agreement for an extended LOA.
