1.1 BACKSTORY
================================================================================
In the late 1980s, Richie "Weazel" Brown, a visionary journalist from Beverly
Hills, saw an opportunity in the media world. Frustrated with the limitations of
traditional networks and their hesitation to push boundaries, Richie relocated to
Los Santos. Using a loan from a shady investor, he built a small but determined
team, founding what would later become Weazel News.

With bold storytelling, cutting-edge visuals, and an unapologetic focus on scandal
and controversy, Weazel News rose quickly. By the mid-1990s, it was a household
name known for:
- Hard-hitting investigations into corruption and crime.
- Sensational live coverage of breaking events.
- Fearless reporting that often shaped public opinion and influenced politics.

Today, Weazel News is the pulse of Los Santos, balancing investigative journalism
with eye-catching broadcasts that keep viewers entertained and informed. What
started as a small vision is now a national powerhouse of media influence.

2.1 MISSION & VISION - WHAT WE DO
================================================================================
Weazel News operates with a clear mission:
- Mission: To deliver fast, bold, and compelling news coverage that shapes the
  conversation of Los Santos.
- Vision: To be the most recognized and trusted media network in San Andreas,
  where people turn first for news, scandal, and entertainment.

Weazel News covers:
- Breaking news (fires, crashes, police chases, riots).
- Entertainment & celebrity gossip.
- Political scandals & corruption exposés.
- Local events, business, and community stories.

Our broadcasts are fast-paced, high energy, and visually captivating, ensuring
audiences are hooked from beginning to end.

2.2 LOCATIONS & FACILITIES
================================================================================
Weazel News operates from several strategically placed facilities:

- 732 Weazel Plaza - Main corporate HQ, executive offices, and primary
  broadcasting studio.
- 662 Weazel News Branch Office - Secondary hub, field team dispatch, and
  backup operations.

All facilities are professional workplaces. Staff are expected to remain
productive, professional, and respectful while on premises.

================================================================================
3.1 GENERAL RULES
================================================================================
- Always be in Voice Chat (VC) while on duty.
- Maintain in-character professionalism at all times.
- Stay on the designated radio frequency during operations.
- Treat all colleagues and command staff with respect.
- Roleplay should remain semi-passive (avoid unnecessary violence).
- Zero tolerance for racism, harassment, or toxicity.
- Do not provoke or encourage non-passive RP outside Weazel News scenes.

================================================================================
3.2 RP GUIDELINES
================================================================================
- Professionalism first: Always portray Weazel News as a legitimate, respected
  media outlet.
- Focus on quality scenes over quantity. A well-done story is better than rushed
  filler.
- Reporters should not harm or endanger others while working.
- Always prioritize good roleplay experiences for the wider community.
- Keep coverage realistic, entertaining, and immersive.

================================================================================
3.3 LOCATION GUIDELINES
================================================================================
- Maintain a professional environment inside all Weazel News properties.
- No loitering, AFK behavior, or disruptive actions at offices.
- Always be working, creating, or preparing content when at HQ.
- Respect property - treat HQ as a workplace, not a hangout.
- Ensure all RP conducted at locations contributes positively to Weazel's image.

4.2 CHAIN OF COMMAND RULES
================================================================================
- Always respect the authority of those higher in the chain of command.
- Disputes should be taken up through proper channels, not in public.
- Orders from higher-ups must be followed unless they directly violate rules.
- Interns and junior staff should treat senior members as mentors.
- COC violations may result in disciplinary action.

================================================================================
5.1 ACTIVITY POLICY
================================================================================
- Minimum requirement: 5 hours per week of active duty.
- Members unable to meet this must file a Reduced Hours Application.
- Failure to meet requirements results in:
  1. Activity Warning
  2. Final Warning
  3. Termination

================================================================================
5.2 DISCIPLINE STRUCTURE
================================================================================
Disciplinary actions are progressive, based on severity:
  1. Verbal Warning   - Minor infractions.
  2. Written Warning  - Repeated or moderate offenses.
  3. Strike           - Serious offenses or repeated poor behavior.
  4. Suspension       - Temporary removal of duties.
  5. Termination      - Permanent removal from Weazel News.

================================================================================
5.3 PROMOTION POLICY
================================================================================
- Minimum of 7 days in your current position.
- Must consistently meet activity requirements.
- Must show professional, high-quality roleplay.
- Promotions are awarded based on merit, dedication, and attitude.

================================================================================
5.4 UNIFORM POLICY
================================================================================
Weazel staff represent the brand and must look professional:
- Approved attire: suits, button-ups, blazers, turtlenecks, skirts, dress shoes.
- No casual streetwear while on duty.
- All uniforms must be clean, respectable, and fit for broadcast.

================================================================================
5.5 VEHICLE POLICY
================================================================================
- Only approved vehicles with Weazel News liveries may be used while on duty.
- Vehicles must be driven responsibly; reckless driving will not be tolerated.
- Personal vehicles should not be used for official news coverage.

================================================================================
6.0 SUMMARY
================================================================================
Weazel News is more than just a workplace - it is a legacy of bold journalism.
Every employee represents the network, both in public and behind the scenes.
Professionalism, dedication, and creativity are the foundation of success here.

If you have questions regarding any part of this SOP, please reach out to Command
Staff. Welcome to the team, and remember:

At Weazel News - if it happens, we cover it.
