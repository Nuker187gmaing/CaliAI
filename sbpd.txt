CALIFORNIA ROLEPLAY - SOUTH BEACH POLICE DEPARTMENT (SBPD)
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP)
California Roleplay - 2026 | Jurisdiction: Los Angeles
================================================================================

SECTION I - INTRODUCTION
================================================================================
This document serves as the official Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) for
the South Beach Police Department (SBPD) within California Roleplay.

All members must follow:
- The Global SOP (GSOP)
- The SBPD SOP

Nothing in this SOP overrides or conflicts with GSOP. Failure to follow
policies may result in disciplinary action.

================================================================================
SECTION II - MISSION STATEMENT
================================================================================
The South Beach Police Department is dedicated to:
- Providing realistic and professional law enforcement roleplay
- Protecting civilians within Los Angeles
- Enforcing the law with integrity, discipline, and accountability

SBPD focuses on structured patrol, strong communication, and high-quality
scenes.

================================================================================

================================================================================
SECTION IV - VEHICLE POLICIES
================================================================================
- Only SBPD-approved vehicles may be used while on duty.
- Vehicles must have realistic colors and liveries.

MAX SPEEDS:
  Standard:           180 MPH
  HEAT Certified:     225 MPH

PURSUIT RULES:
- Must prioritize public safety.
- No reckless or unrealistic driving.
- Disengage if unsafe.
- No excessive off-roading.

ADDITIONAL:
- Vehicles must remain locked.
- No use of tactical vehicles unless authorized.
- No visor + lightbar combo unless supervisor.

================================================================================
SECTION V - UNIFORM POLICIES
================================================================================
- Must wear rank-appropriate uniforms at all times.
- Uniforms must remain professional and realistic.

NOT PERMITTED:
- Bright or unrealistic hair colors.
- Neck tattoos.

FORMAL UNIFORMS REQUIRED FOR:
- Briefings
- Ceremonial events

================================================================================
SECTION VI - WEAPON STRUCTURE & POLICIES
================================================================================
- Only department-issued weapons are allowed.
- All weapons must follow Use of Force guidelines.

RESTRICTIONS:
- No illegal weapons.
- No incendiary or explosive rounds.
- No mag dumping.

TACTICAL WEAPONS:
- Only authorized for SWAT / Tactical units.
- Must be certified to use.

================================================================================
SECTION VII - ACTIVITY REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================
- Members must meet monthly activity expectations.
- Must remain active in patrol, trainings, and department events.

FAILURE TO MEET ACTIVITY:
- Requires LOA submission.
- Or may result in removal.

================================================================================
SECTION VIII - LOA POLICIES (LEAVE OF ABSENCE)
================================================================================
- LOA must be submitted if inactive for extended periods.
- LOA exceeding 30 days may result in removal unless approved.

RULES:
- Must include reason and duration.
- Abuse of LOA may result in punishment.

================================================================================
SECTION IX - PUNISHMENT GUIDELINES
================================================================================

STRIKE SYSTEM:
  Strike 1 - Warning
  Strike 2 - Suspension
  Strike 3 - Termination

DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS (PROGRESSIVE):
  1. Verbal Warning
  2. Written Warning
  3. Suspension
  4. Demotion
  5. Termination

NOTES:
- All punishments must be documented and supported with evidence.

================================================================================
SECTION X - SUBDIVISIONS
================================================================================

SRT (TAC-1) - Special Response Team:
  Handles high-risk situations. Requires certification.

GANG UNIT (GANG-1):
  Focuses on gang-related crime.

AIR UNIT (AIR-1):
  Provides aerial support.

K9 UNIT (K9-1):
  Assists with searches and tracking.

TRAFFIC ENFORCEMENT (TEU-1):
  Handles traffic enforcement and pursuits.

UNDERCOVER UNIT (UC-1):
  Conducts covert investigations.

ELIGIBILITY FOR SUBDIVISIONS:
- Must meet rank requirements.
- Must meet activity standards.
- Must complete required training.

================================================================================
SECTION XI - PROMOTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================
Promotions are based on:
- Activity
- Performance
- Professionalism
- Time in rank

RULES:
- 1-rank promotion: 7-day cooldown.
- 2-rank promotion: 14-day cooldown.
- Promotions must be earned, not given.

================================================================================
SECTION XII - NAMING POLICY (IN-GAME & DISCORD)
================================================================================

IN-GAME:
- Must follow First and Last Name format.
- Acceptable formats: Adam R. / A. Ross

DISCORD:
- Must include callsign and rank (if required).

RESTRICTIONS:
- No unrealistic or troll names.

================================================================================
SECTION XIII - IDENTIFYING WHILE ON DUTY
================================================================================
- Must have correct headtag (badge) and proper callsign.
- Identify yourself when asked: name, badge number, and callsign.
- Failure to identify may result in punishment.

================================================================================
SECTION XIV - CAD POLICIES
================================================================================
- Must be in CAD at all times while on duty.
- Must have a valid character profile with no criminal record.

STATUS UPDATES:
- Must update 10-8 (active) and 10-7 (off duty) in CAD.
- Failure to use CAD properly may result in discipline.

================================================================================
SECTION XV - USE OF FORCE
================================================================================
SBPD follows the standard continuum:
  1. Verbal Commands
  2. Control Techniques
  3. Non-Lethal (Taser)
  4. Lethal Force

RULES:
- Force must be reasonable and justified.
- Must stop when threat is neutralized.

================================================================================
SECTION XVI - RTO ETIQUETTE
================================================================================

GENERAL RULES:
- No talking over others.
- Keep comms clear.

WITH DISPATCH:
- Request permission before speaking.

WITHOUT DISPATCH:
- Follow chain of command.

IMPORTANT:
- No OOC in RTO.
- No calling FailRP over radio.
- Use 10-codes when possible.

================================================================================
FINAL STATEMENT
================================================================================
By joining SBPD, you agree to:
- Follow all Global SOP and SBPD SOP.
- Maintain professionalism at all times.
- Provide realistic and high-quality roleplay.

SBPD is built on structure, discipline, and consistency. Every member is
expected to uphold these standards.
