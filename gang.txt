SECTION 1 | INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

1.01 | EXPECTATIONS
This document outlines the basic elements of the gang community, rules, structure,
and guidelines to follow. The principles of the Gang Community are meant to act as
guidance for quality of roleplay and to represent the Gang Community in the best
manner.

- Members must prioritize high-quality roleplay aligned with immersion, realism,
  and creativity.
- Actions taken by gang members must be justifiable within roleplay context, NOT
  solely driven by personal gain or out-of-character motivations.
- Players are responsible for maintaining a balance between hostile and passive RP,
  ensuring all engagements remain fair, story-driven, and enjoyable for all parties.
- Respect for server rules, staff, and other community members is a foundational
  rule. Gang Roleplay does NOT excuse toxicity, harassment, or rule-breaking.
- Members are held to a higher standard of conduct, both in-character and
  out-of-character.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.02 | GANG COORDINATION
Gang Coordinators play a critical role in maintaining structure, quality, and
integrity of gang roleplay. Their responsibilities:
- Supervise gang activity across their respective categories (street gangs, cartels,
  motorcycle clubs, crime families, militias).
- Ensure consistency, creativity, and adherence to server standards.
- Actively participate in roleplay when appropriate; offer guidance and support.
- Facilitate engaging storylines that benefit the larger community.
- Serve as liaisons between individual gangs and Gang Management.
- Assist gang leadership with administrative duties.
- Help interpret and enforce community guidelines.
- Step in to resolve disputes or concerns before they escalate.
- Revoke invalid punishments and reinstate unjust terminations (subject to Gang
  Coordination Structure).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.03 | CHAIN OF COMMAND
  Gang Overseer          - Oversees community operations; final authority
  Gang Director          - Handles day-to-day operations
  Assistant Gang Director - Assists the Gang Director with gang operations
  Gang Manager           - Serves as lead for Gang Coordinators
  Assistant Gang Manager  - Serves as lead of category
  Senior Gang Coordinator
  Gang Coordinator
  Trial Gang Coordinator

SECTION 2 | GANG GUIDELINES
================================================================================

2.01 | GANG RECRUITMENT
Gangs can be joined in-game or through Discord. The recruitment-hub channel in the
Gang Community Discord lists all joinable gangs.

CATEGORY LIMITS - You may be in a MAXIMUM of 4 gangs across the 4 categories:
  - 1 Cartel OR Militia (cannot be in both at the same time)
  - 1 Motorcycle Club
  - 1 Crime Family
  - 1 Street Gang

You CANNOT be in 2+ gangs within the same category (e.g., 2 cartels, 2 MCs, etc.)

PAROLE SYSTEM - If you leave a gang:
- Below High Command: 3 days of parole for that category
- High Command: 5 days of parole for that category
- Parole applies per category (e.g., leaving a cartel = Cartel/Militia parole)
- It is the duty of gang command to verify recruits are not on active parole.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.02 | GANG TIERS
Gangs are divided into tiers determined by the Gang Management Team, based on:
stature, activity, tenure, quality of roleplay, and experience.

TIER 3: Highest tier — top of each category; should be most feared.
TIER 2: Mid tier.
TIER 1: Entry tier — new gangs. A Tier 1 gang should NOT provoke or raid Tier 3 gangs.

TIER 3 PERK: High Command of Tier 3 gangs may clock-in as staff while on as that
gang. This privilege can be removed for abuse of staff permissions.

FEAR HIERARCHY TABLE (rows = tier, columns = category):
  Tier 3 Cartels/Militias > Tier 3 MCs > Tier 3 Crime Families > Tier 3 Street Gangs
  Tier 2 Cartels/Militias > Tier 2 MCs > Tier 2 Crime Families > Tier 2 Street Gangs
  Tier 1 Cartels/Militias > Tier 1 MCs > Tier 1 Crime Families > Tier 1 Street Gangs

If a gang is ABOVE you in this hierarchy, they should be feared. If you are on the
same row, you are of equal stature and fear does not apply.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.03 | GANG ACTIVITY AND REQUIREMENTS
MINIMUM ACTIVITY: 100 hours per month to maintain gang status.
- New gangs receive a 1 calendar month grace period before requirements are enforced.
- Falling below 100 hours = inactivity warning (valid for 14 days).
- If activity not increased after warning = gang shutdown (slot, tag, and free
  locations forfeited).

MERGING:
- Gangs may merge after a 30-day cooldown from launch.
- NO merges within 30 days of launching.
- Upon merging: highest position offered is the highest supervisor (non-command) rank.
- No command positions offered in merges.
- Gang owner forfeits gang slot, gangtag, and free locations upon merging.

GANG STRIKE SYSTEM (strikes issued for 30 days):
  Gang Strike #1 - Suspension of Clock-In Permissions (Tier 3)
  Gang Strike #2 - Demotion / Suspension
  Gang Strike #3 - Gang Shutdown

Determination of gang strikes is at the discretion of Gang Management (individual
member vs. whole gang punishment based on command involvement).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.04 | RANK GUIDELINES
Gangs MUST adhere to these rank guidelines. Violations result in severe punishment
for the command member responsible, and potentially the whole gang.

INSTATEMENT LIMIT: Highest rank anyone can be instated WITHOUT Gang Coordinator
approval is the highest non-command rank.

TIME IN RANK REQUIREMENTS:
  Supervisor rank:  Must wait 7 days before eligible for promotion
  Low Command rank: Must wait 14 days before eligible for promotion to High Command

- Failure to follow time-in-rank requirements = member demoted back down with rank
  reset + punishment for the command member who made the movement.
- Placements into command from reserves require Gang Coordination Team approval.
- New/starting gangs have 30 days from initial launch to fill command ranks without
  approval.

RANK DEFINITIONS (for standardization):
  High Command: Ranks 00-05
  Low Command:  Ranks 06-10

DISCORD NAME PREFIXES:
  High Command: may have # before the name
  Low Command:  may have $ before the name

Subdivisions are subject to approval by the Gang Coordination Team and names must
comply with TOS standards.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.05 | GANG TRANSFERS
Transfers are open 24/7, handled by the Gang Coordination Team through the ticket
system in the Gang Discord.

TRANSFER RULES:
- You can ONLY transfer between gangs within your category division:
  (Cartels/Militias, Motorcycle Clubs, Crime Families, Street Gangs)
- NO transfers between different category divisions (e.g., Cartel to Street Gang).

TRANSFER RANK DEMOTION TABLE:
  (Rows = Originating gang tier | Columns = Target gang tier)

  Originating Tier 3 -> Target Tier 3: 1 rank demotion
  Originating Tier 3 -> Target Tier 2: 0 rank demotion
  Originating Tier 3 -> Target Tier 1: 0 rank demotion
  Originating Tier 2 -> Target Tier 3: 2 rank demotion
  Originating Tier 2 -> Target Tier 2: 1 rank demotion
  Originating Tier 2 -> Target Tier 1: 0 rank demotion
  Originating Tier 1 -> Target Tier 3: 3 rank demotion
  Originating Tier 1 -> Target Tier 2: 2 rank demotion
  Originating Tier 1 -> Target Tier 1: 1 rank demotion

NOTE: Command transfers are at the discretion of Gang Coordination and the target
gang's High Command; the above table does NOT apply to command transfers.

TRANSFER REQUIREMENTS:
  - 30 days in current gang
  - Must be active within current gang
  - No severe punishment history
  - No active punishments
  - Cannot be the first rank in your gang

SECTION 3 | GANG INTERACTIONS
================================================================================

3.01 | GANG ALLIANCES
Limitations are placed on gang alliances to maintain balance, promote diverse
roleplay, and prevent power-stacking.

KEY RULES:
- All gang relationships MUST be formally defined in the Gang Relations system in
  the Gang Discord using /relations set by Gang High Command.
- Informal or undeclared alliances are NOT recognized in RP or staff review.
- Cartels and Militias that ally with Street Gangs may NOT interject or assist in
  street-gang related beef (turf wars, retaliations, shootouts, backup).
- Cross-category alliances may exist for political, economic, or narrative RP, but
  they do NOT override category boundaries.

DEFENSIVE ALLIANCES (within same category):
- Multiple gangs within the same category may temporarily ally to withstand pressure
  from a higher-level or higher-tier category.
  Example: Two Street Gangs may ally defensively against an MC or Militia.
- These alliances may NOT be used offensively or within their own category.
- Two Tier 3 Street Gangs may NOT use an alliance to dominate or bully another
  Street Gang of similar or lower tier.
- Same-category alliances should exist to BALANCE power, not to exploit it.

RIVALRIES:
- Must be consistent, ongoing, and reflective of declared relationships and tier
  hierarchy.
- Lower-tier gangs engaging higher-tier gangs must do so realistically with proper
  RP justification or alliances.

COMMANDS:
- /relations set  - Declare alliance/rivalry (High Command only)
- /relations view  - View current relationships (any gang member)

Failure to properly declare alliances, bypass restrictions, or use alliances to
power-stack may result in administrative action.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.02 | BEEF STAGES
Gang interactions are determined by their relationship stage. Gangs MUST progress
through stages in order — jumping to a higher stage without going through previous
stages is NOT permitted.

Beef stages are discussed in the #beef-request channel between the High Command of
both gangs before being made official and approved by the Gang Coordination Team.

CARTELS & MILITIAS BEEF STAGES:

Stage 1:
  - Violent aggression
  - Use of weapons (within gun rules)
  - Raiding properties (5 minutes prior RP required)
  - All raids are boots on ground
  - Allies CANNOT be involved
  - Must remain in Stage 1 for 1 week

Stage 2:
  - Violent aggression
  - Use of weapons (within gun rules)
  - Raiding all properties (with GME)
  - Allies CANNOT be involved
  - Boots-on-ground subject to agreement by both parties
  - Must remain in Stage 2 for 1 week

Stage 3:
  - Free locations can be put up for grabs at turf wars (must be agreed by both)
  - All-out war
  - Boots-on-ground subject to agreement by both parties
  - KOS on sight (if marked)
  - Allies CAN be involved

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CRIME FAMILIES, STREET GANGS & MOTORCYCLE CLUBS BEEF STAGES:

Stage 1:
  - Verbal aggression
  - Melee weapons
  - Theft of assets (outside of gang properties)
  - Allies CANNOT be involved
  - Must remain in Stage 1 for 1 week

Stage 2:
  - Violent aggression
  - Use of weapons (within gun rules)
  - Raiding properties
  - All conflicts are boots-on-ground (NO shooting from inside a vehicle)
  - Allies CANNOT be involved
  - Must remain in Stage 2 for 1 week

Stage 3:
  - Free locations can be put up for grabs at turf wars (must be agreed by both)
  - All-out war
  - Boots-on-ground subject to agreement by both parties
  - Kill on sight (if marked)
  - Allies CAN be involved
