SECTION 1.0 | INTRODUCTION
================================================================================
BSE is the official department-level SOP within CaliRP, compliant with the GSOP.
Where this document is silent, the GSOP controls. If a conflict is identified,
the stricter rule applies and GSOP prevails.

SCOPE: Applies to all BSE personnel (Probationary through Director) while on or
representing the department. BSE operates as a California-based Federal Agency
focusing on anti-heavy crimes (bank robberies, homicide/murder, serial & mass
violence, organized robbery crews, high-value kidnappings, terrorism indicators)
and supports normal LEO functions in accordance with GSOP.

AUTHORITY: BSE Agents are federal LEO within the roleplay environment. Jurisdiction
is state-wide with interagency support to local LEO and DHS. Traffic enforcement
limitations for Federal units per GSOP remain in effect (see Section 10.0).

================================================================================
SECTION 1.1 | MISSION STATEMENT
================================================================================
Protect California and its communities by detecting, disrupting, and dismantling
violent and organized criminal threats through intelligence-led investigations,
decisive enforcement, and professional interagency coordination.

OBJECTIVES:
- Rapidly respond to and manage critical incidents (bank/armored-car robberies,
  active killer events, hostage/barricade scenarios).
- Conduct major case investigations into homicide, organized robbery/crew activity,
  weapons trafficking, and RICO-style conspiracies.
- Lead or augment multi-agency task forces, ensuring lawful tactics and safe
  resolutions.
- Uphold public trust through accountability, integrity, and compliance with GSOP.

================================================================================
SECTION 1.2 | PRELIMINARY INFORMATION
================================================================================
- Department of Homefront Security (DHS): Enforces GSOP server-wide. BSE

- CaliRP Penal Codes: BSE adopts and enforces 2024 Penal Codes and speed limits.

SECTION 2.0 | DEPARTMENT STRUCTURE
================================================================================

2.1 DEPARTMENTS BY CATEGORY:
BSE is a Main Federal Department within the GSOP structure. BSE participates in
global policies including TAC, UC, border, and FAA rules (Section 9.0).

2.2 CHAIN OF COMMAND:

01 - Director
  Head of the Bureau; oversees all BSE operations, divisions, policies, and
  interagency relations. Answers directly to Department Coordination / DOJ.
  Ultimate authority on disciplinary actions and command appointments.

02 - Deputy Director
  Second-in-command of the Bureau. Supervises Assistant Directors, approves
  federal task force operations, manages internal affairs. Acts as Director
  in their absence.

03 - Assistant Director
  Manages operational oversight of major divisions (Operations, Investigations,
  Intelligence). Liaison between field command and upper administration.
  Ensures GSOP and federal standards are enforced bureau-wide.

04 - Regional Director
  Oversees operations within a designated region of California (North, Central,
  South). Responsible for inter-regional coordination, recruitment oversight,
  and local agency partnerships.

05 - Division Chief
  Leads a specific division (Tactical Enforcement, Major Crimes, Intelligence,
  etc.). Reports directly to the Assistant or Regional Director. Maintains
  division SOPs, rosters, and certification tracking.

06 - Chief of Staff (THC)
  Coordinates all internal communications, scheduling, and administrative policy.
  Direct advisor to the Director and Deputy Director. Oversees internal HR,
  documentation, and coordination tasks.

07 - Section Chief (LC)
  Leads multiple units or squads within a division. Manages supervisors and SACs
  under their section. Approves tactical activations and field deployments.

08 - Special Agent in Charge (SAC)
  Commands a field office or operational district. Oversees all cases, raids,
  and local Bureau activity within their jurisdiction. Reports directly to the
  Section Chief.

09 - Deputy Special Agent in Charge (DSAC)
  Second-in-command to the SAC; acts in their absence. Supervises unit
  Supervisors and ensures compliance with Bureau procedures.

10 - Supervisor (LC)
  Directly manages field teams, assigns cases, monitors investigations, and
  handles daily operations. Responsible for evaluating and training new or
  probationary agents.

11 - Lead Special Agent (TLC)
  Senior operational leader; assists Supervisors in field coordination and
  training. Acts as shift or scene lead during high-risk operations. May
  temporarily assume supervisory responsibilities when designated.

12 - Senior Special Agent (SSA)
  Highly experienced investigator. Leads critical cases, mentors junior agents,
  and may head small task groups. Reports to Supervisor or Lead Special Agent.

13 - Special Agent (SA)
  Standard field agent rank. Conducts investigations, assists in tactical
  operations, prepares case files, and executes warrants. Represents the
  backbone of BSE enforcement.

14 - Field Agent (FA)
  Transitional stage between Agent and Special Agent. Gathers field experience,
  assists with arrests, and supports investigations under supervision.

15 - Agent (AGT)
  Fully certified BSE operative. Executes assignments as part of a team; still
  under mentorship of senior staff.

16 - Probationary Agent / Trainee (PA)
  Entry-level recruit currently in or recently graduated from training academy.
  Must complete all certifications (Firearms, Federal Procedures, GSOP
  Compliance, and FTO evaluation). May NOT operate solo or lead investigations
  until cleared.

COMMAND SPOTS: BSE adheres to GSOP cap of 25 total command (HC+THC+LC).
Preferred layout: 5 HC, 4 THC, 12 LC.

SECTION 3.0 | IN-GAME RULES
================================================================================

3.1 IN CHARACTER (IC):
BSE adopts GSOP Section 3.1 in full, including: 2-life rule, no breaking
character, adherence to penal codes, internal punishments, use of force, and
weapon policies. Tactical role separation (Scene Command vs Negotiator vs Tac
Lead) is mandatory.

3.2 DUTY BLIPS & HEADTAGS (GSOP-Aligned):
- Marked:     Blip ON | Headtag ON
- Unmarked:   Blip ON | Headtag Optional (BSE requires visible identification
              when engaging enforcement)
- Undercover: Blip ON | Headtag OFF (follow UC procedures; must re-tag on
              contact/arrival per Section 9.2)

3.3 OUT OF CHARACTER (OOC):
BSE adopts GSOP Section 3.2 entirely (strikes, LOA rules, respect toward
departments, follow staff, professionalism, RTO etiquette, etc.).

SECTION 4.0 | RADIO TRAFFIC ONLY (RTO) PROFESSIONALISM
================================================================================

4.1 10-CODES:
Use the server's official 10-codes. If unknown, use plain English equivalents.
"Shots fired" may be used for 10-13.

4.2 COMMUNICATING IN RTO:
- With Dispatch: request to transmit; supply concise, relevant details.
- Without Dispatch: self-dispatch per GSOP; still obey RTO authority.
- Use 10-1 for side-channel comms as needed; keep CAD statuses updated.
- Request Dispatch readiness before a 10-11 callout (if assisting locals).
- Do NOT call out FailRP/VDM/RDM in RTO; use tickets/staff.
- Stay realistic (no "talking while down").

4.3 AUTHORITY IN RTO:
BSE follows GSOP roles for Supervisors, Pre-Main HC, Main HC, and Dispatch.
Chain of Command must be followed at all times. Briefings require Main Dept HC
or DHS S.S.A.+ per GSOP.

SECTION 5.0 | WEAPON, UNIFORM & VEHICLE POLICIES
================================================================================

5.1 GENERAL INFORMATION:
- Department equipment is for BSE personnel only; carry valid licenses.
- Treat all displayed firearms as lethal; no mag-dumping; cease fire when
  threat ends.
- Flashbangs: SRT-only or certified tactical use when necessary.
- Explosives restricted except approved breaching (per GSOP & TAC rules).

5.2 VEHICLES (GSOP-COMPLIANT):
- Realistic LEO liveries/colors only.
- Speed limits: 180 mph max (or 225 mph if HEAT/TEU certified) and
  weather-dependent; disengage if unsafe.
- FEDERAL TRAFFIC STOP LIMITATION: BSE will NOT initiate routine traffic stops.
  Exceptions:
  1. At local request/coordination
  2. Felony in progress (imminent threat to life)
  3. Drive-by/active violent felony observed (engage, then handoff)
- Lock vehicles; follow department plate formats per GSOP
  (2N-3L-3N or 2N-2L-4N, professional content).
- Tactical vehicles (Bearcat/MRAP): deployment only, no patrol.
- Window tint: federal may use up to "Dark Smoke," never full blackout.
- Supervisors may use visor lights; patrol uses lightbar unless exempt.

5.3 WEAPONS:
- Use only BSE-authorized weapons and attachments.
- Heavy weapons limited to SRT/active tactical.
- No illegal weapons (AP Pistol, Ghost Glock, etc.).
- No incendiary/explosive ammo.

5.4 UNIFORM STANDARDS:
- Rank-appropriate uniforms; professional grooming; no neck/face tattoos.
- Formal uniforms for RTO briefings or press releases.
- Acceptable duty options:
  * Suit + Badge (formal)
  * Tactical Polo & Plate Carrier (Operations)
  * Full SRT Kit (tactical activation with "AGENT/BSE" marking)

SECTION 8.0 | DEPARTMENT POLICIES (BSE-SPECIFIC)
================================================================================

8.1 DEPARTMENT COMMAND:
- HC Range (01-05) per GSOP. Pre-Main/DoD concurrency limits apply.
- Promotions must respect GSOP cooling periods; 3-rank+ changes require LDC
  approval.
- HC must maintain 30+ hours/month and demonstrate time-in-rank.

8.2 DEPARTMENT DISCIPLINE:
- BSE enforces CaliRP Global Disciplinary Guidelines 1.0.
- All misconduct by LEO (any dept) observed by BSE must be reported via DHS
  or proper ticket.
- HC strikes count as Department Strikes and are escalated to Department
  Director Team.

8.3 DEPARTMENT REQUIREMENTS (DOCUMENTATION):
- Master Roster (employees/vehicles/uniforms)
- Clear Rank Structure; this SOP; classroom & field training guides
- All docs owned by ldc@calirp.gg or department director per GSOP
- Ownership Clause: All BSE docs belong to the department; altering/removal
  post-resignation is prohibited.

8.4 PUNISHMENT OF MEMBERS:
Progressive discipline per GSOP:
Oral Warning -> Written Warning -> Suspension -> Demotion -> Termination
All proof must be logged in the BSE Discord evidence channel.

8.4.1 WHAT COULD GET YOU SUSPENDED (not limited to):
- Disrespecting other departments
- Wrongly discharging your firearm
- Using deadly force on a cooperative suspect
- Abuse of Sirens & Lights
- Abusing your authority
- Use of explosives on non-bulletproof vehicles

8.4.2 SUSPENSION LIMITATIONS - While suspended you MUST refrain from:
1. Clocking on duty
2. Joining RTO
3. Patrolling
4. Hosting trainings
5. Assisting trainings
6. Hosting ride-alongs
7. Participating in Arrests / Raids

8.5 TRAINING & RECRUITMENT:
- Required pipeline: Application -> Interview -> Training (Academy & FTO)
- Open Recruitments per GSOP cadence/notice; adherence to event caps.
- Trainers must use standardized evaluation rubrics.
- Core BSE Curriculum (minimum):
  1. GSOP compliance & ethics
  2. Use-of-Force & de-escalation
  3. High-Risk Incident Command (HRI-ICS)
  4. Bank Robbery/Hostage Protocols
  5. Homicide & Major Crimes Case Building
  6. SRT foundations (room entries, breaching, L&S driving, TEMS overview)
  7. UC/Surveillance tradecraft (legalities, safety)
  8. Evidence, warrants, and chain of custody
  9. CAD, report writing, press/PIO basics

8.6 SUBDIVISION POLICIES:
BSE may operate GSOP-conforming subdivisions (TBA). Subdivision leads must be
Supervisor+ and maintain SOPs, rosters, and training packets. TAC activation
rules in Section 9.1 apply (min operators, no patrol, vehicle limits).

8.7 LEO TRAFFIC STOPS (BSE APPLICATION):
BSE adopts GSOP Section 8.7. BSE HC may pull over BSE units internally.
Cross-department LEO stops require DHS per GSOP.

8.8 CORRUPT ROLEPLAY:
Absolutely prohibited (bribes, torture, etc.). Violations result in removal
across departments as outlined in GSOP Section 8.8.

8.9 BADGE & NAME FORMATS:
- Headtag must be active when conducting law enforcement actions or upon
  arrival on-scene (UC exceptions until contact).
- BSE Callsign Format: BSE-### (assigned by BSE Command). Example: BSE-127
- RTO Display: BSE-### | J. Doe
  When undercover: 127 | UC-1 | J. Doe (per Section 9.2)
- Unit Plates: per GSOP formats in Section 5.2 (no unprofessional content)

SECTION 9.0 | GLOBAL POLICIES (ADOPTED BY BSE)
================================================================================

9.1 TAC POLICIES (BSE SRT):
- SRT responds to robberies, hostages, 10-13s, high-priority calls, and
  illegal org actions per GSOP.
- ACTIVATION REQUIREMENTS:
  * 3+ operators to activate; low-TAC minimum 2 (low-priority)
  * Clearly designated Scene Command, Negotiator, and Tac Lead (distinct roles)
  * Markings ("AGENT"/"BSE SRT") on vests unless JSOC exception (DoD only)
  * Vehicle usage and militarized restrictions enforced; no SRT patrol
  * No kill-confirming/mag-dumping
  * Callsign: TAC-1/SRT-1 for team activation

9.2 UNDERCOVER (UC):
- Must be on-duty and follow all laws; no tasting drugs, no real-world gang
  affiliations.
- Maintain fake ID; reveal badge to LEO upon enforcement interactions.
- Headtag OFF while covert; re-tag when engaging LEO action/arrival.
- Update RTO to show UC activation (e.g., 201 | UC-1 | J. Doe).
- UC will NOT respond to routine 10-11/10-80/10-13 unless tied to UC mission.

9.3 BORDER:
BSE only assists if DHS/USBP is present or on request; follows GSOP search
lanes, use-of-force, and vehicle policies. No shooting at crossers unless
threat criteria met.

9.4 FAA (AIR OPERATIONS):
- AIR-1 may land where realistic; no mounted weapon use; small-arms only if
  engaged first (TAC exception for vehicle-disable on HVTs as GSOP allows).
- Weather & altitude restrictions apply.
- Request Zancudo clearance on Radio 149 as applicable.

SECTION 14.0 | LOA POLICY
================================================================================

14.1 PURPOSE:
The BSE Leave of Absence (LOA) ensures all personnel maintain transparency,
accountability, and professionalism when temporarily stepping away from duties.
This policy ensures operational stability, prevents inactivity issues, and allows
members to return without disciplinary action when proper procedure is followed.

14.2 REQUESTING AN LOA:
- All LOAs must be formally requested through the BSE Discord LOA Request Channel
  or via support ticket.
- LOA requests MUST include:
  1. Name & Callsign
  2. Rank & Sub-division (if applicable)
  3. Start & End dates
  4. Reason (generalized if private)
  5. Expected return date

Failure to submit an LOA before extended inactivity may result in an inactivity
strike or termination per GSOP Section 3.2.

SECTION 16.0 | CAD (COMPUTER AIDED DISPATCH) POLICY
================================================================================

16.1 PURPOSE:
Defines proper usage, expectations, and enforcement of CAD systems for all BSE
Agents. CAD serves as the official record-keeping and accountability tool for:
1. Tracking call assignment & responses
2. Unit statuses (10-8, 10-7, 10-23, etc.)
3. Arrests, citations, and reports
4. Warrants & BOLOs
5. Proper documentation

16.2 SCOPE:
This policy applies to ALL BSE personnel regardless of rank, division, or
assignment. It governs CAD use in patrols, investigations, tac activations, and
interagency task forces. No Agent is exempt from CAD usage unless specifically
authorized by the Director or Deputy Director under exceptional operational
circumstances (e.g., covert operations per Section 9.2 UC Guidelines).

16.3 CAD LOGIN REQUIREMENTS:
- Agents MUST log into CAD before going 10-8 or entering RTO.
- Login PRIOR to /duty
- Maintain accurate status updates:
  * 10-8  = On Duty / Available
  * 10-6  = Busy / In Operation
  * 10-7  = Out of Service / Break
  * 10-23 = On Scene
  * 10-41 = Starting Tour of Duty
  * 10-42 = Ending Tour of Duty

APPENDIX A | BSE HIGH-RISK PLAYBOOKS
================================================================================

A1. BANK/ARMORED-CAR ROBBERY (LIVE):
1. Initial Action: Containment (inner/outer perimeter), dispatch RTO split,
   establish Scene Command.
2. Intel: Cameras/drone/AIR-1 overwatch, suspect count, arms, hostages, vehicle
   description, escape routes.
3. Negotiation: Time, distance, cover; extract hostages; demand management;
   crisis log.
4. Tactical Options: SRT staging, ERPs, breaching criteria, sniper containment
   (certified only), vehicle interdiction box.
5. Transition: Arrest/intercept teams; evidence preservation; PIO statement;
   after-action packet (AAR within 24 hours).

A2. HOMICIDE/MAJOR CRIMES:
1. Scene: Life-saving first; secure scene; log entries; assign Evidence/Sketch.
2. Leads: Victimology; canvass; video dragnet; BOLOs; NIBIN/ballistics.
3. Case: Warrant workflow; interview plan; informant handling; conspiracy chart.
4. Arrest: Low-risk pickup vs SRT warrant; safety plan; post-arrest interview.
5. Closure: D.A. coordination; case file QA; press posture.

A3. ACTIVE KILLER/HOSTAGE:
ICS roles separated; rescue task force with locals; hall-boss; warm-zone triage;
comms brevity; med handoff.

A4. UC/SURVEILLANCE:
Cover ID package; contact report cadence; dead-drop/device handling;
burn-criteria & extraction; immediate re-tagging on enforcement.

A5. EVIDENCE & REPORTING:
Chain of custody forms; digital evidence naming; disclosure packet to DOJ;
24h initial report / 72h full report.
