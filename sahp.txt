CHAPTER 1 - LAW ENFORCEMENT CODE OF ETHICS
================================================================================

1.1 - RESPONSIBILITIES:
All Troopers of the San Andreas Highway Patrol are expected to:
1. Set targets and goals; be ambitious within the department and server.
2. Be proficient in laws and regulations pertaining to Trooper duties.
3. Accept constructive criticism for growth and enhancement.
4. Identify and mitigate situations that put themselves or others in harm's way.
5. Use force appropriately, never outside permissions of the law or this document.
6. Interview witnesses/victims, investigate crimes, apprehend criminals, make arrests.
7. Attend weekly Department-wide meetings to stay updated.
8. Conduct themselves kindly and cautiously. Do not steal, do not cut corners.
   If a yes hasn't been explicitly said, assume a no.
9. Know and remember Miranda rights:
   "You have the right to remain silent. If you choose to waive this right, then
   anything you say can and will be used against you in a court of law. You have
   the right to have an attorney present during questioning; if you cannot afford
   an attorney, then the state will provide one for you. Do you understand these
   rights as they have been read to you?"

1.2 - CORE VALUES:
- Safety: Protect those who travel and live in the state of California.
- Service: Provide professional service to the public; open communication; dedicate
  to improving quality of life for all residents and visitors.
- Security: Protect the public, their property, state employees, and state infrastructure.

1.3 - COMMISSIONER'S OFFICE GOALS:
1. Ensure all policies and procedures are in the best interest of safety/well-being.
2. Exhibit a compassionate, responsive attitude toward the community.
3. Assist the public in the utmost professional manner with empathy and integrity.
4. Familiarize with all community training materials, department policy memorandums,
   SOPs, CaliRP Community Rules. Ignorance is NOT a defense against disciplinary action.
5. Use knowledge and experience to maintain order between all operations.
6. Guide and train all Troopers with the correct instruction for success.

1.4 - SAHP DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:
1. Primary function: preserve peace, protect life and property, prevent crime,
   apprehend criminal suspects, recover lost/stolen property, enforce traffic and
   civil laws from Penal Codes fairly and impartially.
2. Be honest and act with integrity. Never abuse authority or powers. Protect human rights.
3. Obey the Penal Codes at all times.
4. Under no circumstances inflict, instigate, or tolerate inhuman or degrading treatment
   to any citizen.
5. Be courteous and civil to the public and fellow Troopers at all times. Exercise
   patience and discretion. Do NOT verbally assault, berate, or verbally abuse the public.
6. Use appropriate language and behavior; do not use offensive language or behavior.
7. Exercise reasonable care to prevent loss of life or damage to property at all times.
8. Justify use of force; all circumstances must be taken into account. Use force
   only if other means are or may be ineffective.
9. Do not make yourself unfit for duty due to alcohol, substances, or misuse of
   prescription drugs (in-character AND out-of-character). Those in violation considered
   for demotion or removal pending investigation.
10. In emergencies where a supervisor is not present, the highest-ranking officer
    present or by radio is in charge.
11. Do not endanger life beyond what is reasonable and necessary. Act in coordination
    with other law enforcement. Do not take unnecessary risks.
12. If a civilian requests a Supervisor on-scene, Troopers may request one or deny
    based on articulation and discretion. When requesting a Supervisor, request the
    lowest-ranking Supervisor first and elevate from there.
13. Keep inter-department communications active at all times. Use SAHP Discord channels
    and radio channels: 2.47 (Main department radio) & 344 (Tactical communications).
    Large-scale incidents: use designated incident channel (50, 51, or 52) chosen by
    the scene commander.

2.2 - CHAIN OF COMMAND:
1. The Chain of Command begins at Corporal (4S-XX). If they cannot assist, they will
   refer you to a higher-ranking member. If a Trooper has an issue with a Chain of
   Command member, contact the next highest or head/assistant head of supervisors.
   Under normal circumstances, Troopers will NOT contact Lieutenants or above directly.
   Failure to abide may result in disciplinary action.
2. Each successive rank means more responsibilities. Maintaining Chain of Command is
   crucial for discipline; lack of discipline can result in casualties or poor service.
3. When a Trooper is promoted to Trial High Command (THC), they also receive the title
   of Assistant Director of Low Command. Resigning from THC results in demotion below THC.

CHAPTER 3 - VEHICLES, UNIFORMS, AND WEAPONS
================================================================================

3.1 - VEHICLE POLICIES:
- Each Trooper gets a new vehicle after every promotion.
- Troopers can drive their rank-allocated vehicle and those below their current rank.
- Exceptions: purchased vehicle pack, active in a subdivision, personal vehicle
  approved by High Command.
- High Command may remove vehicle access as punishment.
- Allowed upgrades: exclude bulletproof tires, custom/unrealistic rims, window tint
  on SAHP department vehicle, or anything that looks unrealistic.
- Vehicles are your office; always lock them to protect weapon systems.
- All vehicles fitted with: radar systems, Mobile Data Terminals, weapons rack, IFAKs.

Specific Rules:
1. All Troopers required to have patrol vehicles with appropriate extras. No Trooper
   shall access a duty vehicle without explicit permission from the owner and SAHP HC.
2. No vehicle pursuits or calls beyond current scene while transporting a detainee.
   Do not operate vehicle dangerously to any detainee's safety.
3. Operate vehicles with due regard to safety at all times, including when using
   emergency signals. Do NOT use emergency signals to evade traffic signals or for
   non-emergency purposes. Slow down going through intersections code 2 or 3.
   Transport suspects code 1 unless high-valued suspect (code 3 permitted).
   Drive like a civilian when patrolling; do not abuse lights/sirens.
4. Extreme circumstances may result in unsafe operation; mitigate danger of any situation.
5. Courtesy rides permitted to stranded individuals who have been identified.
   Troopers MUST identify all transportees prior to entry.
6. Troopers may choose not to transport citizens unless ordered by a supervisor or
   federal agents.
7. Do NOT use vehicle as weapon against foot pedestrians (exception: extreme high-priority
   circumstances where vehicle is most practical). Report to supervisor immediately when
   vehicle is used to ram any pedestrian or vehicle. NEVER ram unarmed pedestrians.
   Minimal contact allowed in foot pursuits at low velocity.
8. Plate format: XXSHPXXX (each X is a number). Only format allowed on duty.
   Exception: unmarked/undercover units.
   - When representing SAHP on duty: duty blips ON, head tags optional.
   - Concert undercover unit: blip tag ON, head tag OFF.
   - Head tag MUST be toggled ON immediately when identifying to other LEOs or
     on large-scale scenes where crossfire threat is imminent.
9. Only use department gang tags (SAHP | Supervisor, SAHP | Traffic Enforcement, etc.)
   unless approved by SAHP High Command.
10. PIT conditions (ALL must be met):
    - Speed: Must be below 130 mph (except TEU advanced tactics certified)
    - Weather: Must not be excessively rainy
    - Traffic: Road around you must be clear of all traffic
    - Pedestrians: No pedestrians or animals in the area of the PIT

3.2 - UNIFORM STRUCTURE:
- Conform to the uniform structure at all times. Violations result in disciplinary action.
- Questions/issues: contact any patrol supervisor.
1. All units MUST wear armored vest during active shooter, shots fired, or high-risk calls.
   Vest may be removed after the call. Optional to patrol with or without vest otherwise.
2. Tattoos permitted but NOT above the neckline. Facial hair permitted; must be kept neat.
   Supervisors reserve the right to enforce "neat in appearance."
3. Trial Low Command and above may use a custom uniform approved by SAHP High Command.
4. Fat and Old Peds are NOT to be utilized while on patrol. Violation = punishment.
3.2.5: Formal uniforms must be followed. Hats are NOT optional at any time. If your
       hair clips through the hat, change your hair so it doesn't clip. (GSOP Compliance)

3.3 - WEAPON STRUCTURE:
1. All Troopers carry: flashlight, baton, X26 taser, combat pistol.
   Only flashlight attachment allowed on service pistol; no custom colors.
2. All Troopers must have pump action shotgun in vehicle weapon rack.
   Must carry M4 Semi-Automatic Rifle in vehicle weapon rack (front row).
   M4: can be fired in single shots, bursts, or rapid fire.
   M4 restrictions: no custom colors, extended magazines, or suppressors.
   All other attachments approved.
3. Weapon systems not listed in 3.3.1 or 3.3.2 are prohibited for routine duty use.
   Exception: extreme necessity (disarmed, fighting to survive). Retreat as soon as possible.
   Supervisors should expedite extraction of Troopers in this situation.

3.4 - IN GAME NAME STRUCTURE:
Format: [Callsign] | [First Name] [Last Initial] or [First Initial] [Last Name]
Example: 1S-01 | Carter G. OR J. Wayne
Must match Discord nickname in CaliRP Discord. Required when actively On-Duty.

CHAPTER 5 - PROBATIONARY CADET INFORMATION AND TRAINING PROGRAM
================================================================================

5.1 - PROBATIONARY CADET INFORMATION:
- All new hires assigned call signs beginning with "PC".
- All probationary cadets MUST be above age 14.
- Cadets are on a strict ONE-STRIKE policy.
- PC Units can be terminated WITHOUT NOTIFICATION for ANY policy violation or
  behavioral issues, by any High or Low Command member.
- Probationary Cadets WILL NOT PATROL ALONE; must ride-along with FTA or FTO.
- Must obey orders of the Trooper they ride-along with at all times, regardless of rank.
- Callsign REQUIRED in steam name during training and patrolling.
- 14 days to complete basic training; 30 days to complete ride-along.
  Failure to meet these deadlines = immediate termination.

5.2 - PROBATIONARY CADET TRAINING PROGRAM:
To advance past PC stage, the Cadet must attend, complete, and pass a Field Training
Event administered by a FTO (announced in #training-announcements in Field Training
Division Channel in SAHP Discord).

Training consists of:
- Classroom: essential information all Troopers must know
- Practical (in-game): use of 10 codes, performing traffic stops, vehicle pursuit policy,
  RTO callouts, vehicle management, basic room/building clearing, firearm tactics.

NOTE: Cadet MUST stay for entire duration. Incomplete attendance = automatic failure.

After training, must complete at least 1 ride-along (minimum 1 hour) with a certified
FTO, plus an evaluation by a Certified FTO.

5.2 - FIELD TRAINING OFFICERS AND ASSISTANTS:
- FTOs are expected to be available to help develop Probationary Cadets.
- Responsible for preparing Cadets for evaluation.
- Must ensure quality of training and instruction.
- Should recognize and propose better ideas for Cadet training to the FTD Head.
- Must be patient and professional at all times.

5.3 - TRAINING COORDINATORS:
- Primary responsibility: assess and manage FTOs and FTAs; monitor trainings for
  fairness and impartiality.
- Assist FTOs/FTAs with questions or concerns.
- Continuously ensure FTOs fill out required documents (logs of all training/ride-alongs).
- To become Training Coordinator: must have participated in multiple trainings and
  ride-alongs, be an expert in all SAHP SOPs, and capable of solving issues.

CHAPTER 6 - PROMOTIONS/DEMOTIONS
================================================================================

6.1 - PROMOTIONS:
- Every rank has minimum requirements. Command team monitors and documents all members
  throughout the promotion period.
- Supervisors and Senior Supervisors can recommend members below their rank.
- High Command reserves the right to hold any member's promotion at their discretion.
- Promotions based on activity and requirements during the previous promotion period.
- For specific requirements, review SAHP Promotional Guidelines.
- Weekly promotion period: Sunday 12am to Saturday 11:59 PM.
  All hours and requirements must be within this period.

6.2 - DISCIPLINARY ACTION:
Low Command and High Command reserve the right to demote, strike, or warn Troopers
for misconduct. Removal requires a majority vote among all Command members.

Reasons for removal:
1. INACTIVITY: Absent more than one week without notifying a direct supervisor.
   May also result in removal of roles/positions or rank demotion.
2. POOR INTRAPERSONAL/PROFESSIONAL INTERACTION: Poor behavior during scenes,
   inability to roleplay legitimately, no intention to roleplay (must be documented).
3. Inability to adhere to department policies and procedures.
4. DISRESPECT of fellow Troopers or any LEO department/entities, including harassment
   in personal messages.
5. All disciplinary actions will NOT be discussed with individuals outside originally
   involved parties.
6. NO PINGING ROLES for any reason except: hosting recruitments, training, or
   sub-division training. Failure = serious disciplinary action.
7. LC members receive warnings for escalating inactivity:
   Strike I = warning | Strike II = demotion | Strike III = dismissal from team.
   LC members must actively engage in discussions, attend meetings, and complete tasks.
8. Department disrespect = severe consequences up to termination.

6.3 - TRANSFER POLICY:
Transfer requests are announced in the CaliRP Discord. If not announced, assume closed.
SAHP reserves the right to refuse transfers.

Transfer may be DENIED if:
1. Current Disciplinary Actions exist.
2. Less than 60 days in SAHP.
3. Not active within last 14 days prior to transfer request.
4. No member may transfer directly into Low Command; placed at highest supervisor
   rank with vacancy.
5. All new transfers, instatements, and reinstatements MUST receive a 14-day
   promotional cooldown. No exceptions.

Transfer Process:
1. Open a Transfer/Resignation Ticket in SAHP Discord to notify High Command.
2. Once requirements verified, open Transfer Ticket in Department Hub.
3. Destination department's HC responds (accept or deny).
4. If accepted: member demoted at least one rank per Global Transfer SOP.
5. Once both HCs accept: current department roles removed; transfer complete.
6. Removed from SAHP master roster and kicked from SAHP Department Discord.

Transfer Disclaimers:
- Destination HC reserves right to deny any transfer request.
- Destination HC may or may not honor subdivision statuses.
- Previous conduct may be discussed between department HCs; may affect onboarding rank.

6.4 - RESIGNATION/RETIREMENT POLICY:
Troopers reserve the right to resign from SAHP. Upon resignation, HC assigns a
2-week cooldown period; cannot apply to any LEO department until cooldown ends.

6.4.1: Command members reserve the right to stay in the Discord upon retirement,
except in cases of termination, transfer, joining another main LEO dept, or ban.
Below SAHP Low Command ranks will be removed from SAHP Discord server.

6.4.2: Eligibility for retired status:
- Provide a two-week written notice to Department HC Team (not required)
- Must not have had any disciplinary sanctions in the last 30 days
- Must leave in a positive standing within the community

6.4.3: "Ret. HC" Role Policy:
- All HC members receive the "Ret. HC" role when retiring from HC position.
- Retired HC maintains their HC role regardless of other positions taken.
- Section 6.4.2 applies to retiring HC members.

6.4.4: "Ret. LC" Role Policy:
- All LC members receive the "Ret. LC" role once they retire from LC position.
- Maintained as long as they are in Discord and NOT active as a trooper.
  (Reserves position is the exception)
- If you come out of retirement, out of reserves, or rejoin Discord, you lose Ret. LC.
- If terminated, transferred, or joined another main LEO Dept, then rejoin SAHP:
  you will NOT maintain your Ret. Role.

6.4.5: Those allowed within SAHP Discord (at HC discretion):
- Reserve Troopers (8S-XX)
- Other Main LEO Department HC
- Retired High Command
- Department Coords or Higher
- DHS Supervisors or Higher
- Friend of SAHP (good standing with SAHP command team, doesn't cause problems)
- State Patrol Command
- Any Department

6.4.6: ANY member within SAHP Discord can be kicked at the Discretion of High Command+.

6.5 - REDUCED HOURS:
Approved by Trial High Command member in extenuating circumstances only.
Personnel must:
- Have been active for the past four weeks
- Be in good standing with the command team
- Have made an impact within the department
Once approved: prohibits any movements within the department; trooper asked to step
down from any command position.

6.5.1: Approved Reduced Hours include exemption from subdivision requirements.
Prohibits any movements within the subdivision; trooper asked to step down from
any subdivision command position.

6.6 - ACTIVITY REQUIREMENTS:
- Master Trooper and above: minimum 12 hours MONTHLY to maintain rank.
- After 30 consecutive days of no reported activity or less than 12 hours in a month:
  inactivity warning issued.
- After 60 days of no activity: removed from SAHP roster and SAHP Discord.
- (Individuals may still be demoted without receiving an inactivity warning)
- From Trooper to Senior Trooper: NO HOUR REQUIREMENT.
  Failure to meet requirements still results in same punishment.

6.6.1: Not required to use gangtags on basic patrol. However, when activating as a
subdivision, you MUST use the required gangtag for that subdivision.
FTD and RD are exempt from gangtags but must post announcements.
TEU, GNTF, SWAT division commanders will enforce gangtag use for members.
CANNOT use Sub-Tags during basic patrol, recruitments, or training; this counts as
fake clocking hours for the subdivision = disciplinary action.

6.7 - LEAVE OF ABSENCE POLICY (LOA):
- All LOA requests must be approved by Low Command.
- While on LOA: cannot go on duty as SAHP or partake in ANY SAHP-related activities
  (including Discord, admin, or paperwork).
- While on LOA: cannot be on another FiveM server as an active LEO.
  Violation = dual planning = termination.
- Must provide a valid reason for LOA to keep track.
- Valid reasons: medical emergency, family emergency, exams, natural disasters,
  vacation, PC issues, or no access to PC/internet.
  NOTE: needing a break is NOT a valid reason.
- If LOA is for a break from FiveM/duties, do NOT perform duties in another department.
- LOA minimum: 7 days; maximum: 14 days (approved by LC member).
- Long-term LOA (15+ days): open Low Command support ticket; High Command must approve.
  HC reserves the right to deny. Depending on length, may be asked to step down from
  command position.
- After LOA ends: 7-day LOA cooldown. Cannot begin another LOA during those 7 days.

6.8 - REINSTATEMENT POLICY:
Requirements:
- Must have resigned within the last 30 days.
- Clean disciplinary record for 30 days before resignation.
- Must be approved by Department High Command.

6.8.1: Rank placed upon reinstatement is at the discretion of Department High Command.

6.8.2: All new transfers, instatements, and reinstatements receive a 14-day promotional
cooldown. During this period, members cannot be promoted. Members must familiarize
themselves with the department structure, expectations, chain of command, and SAHP SOP.

6.9 - LC RANK GUIDELINES (TENURE LIMITS):
- Colonels: may NOT retain rank more than 90 days
- Lieutenant Colonels: may NOT retain rank more than 60 days
- Commanders and Majors: may NOT retain rank more than 50 days
- Captains and Lieutenant Commanders: may NOT retain rank more than 40 days
- Trial Low Command: may NOT retain rank more than 4 weeks

Failure to adhere may result in activity review and potential removal from Low Command.
Exceptions: active status with rank lock (authorized by Commissioner's office) or no
available space in command structure.

All transitions into/out of command undergo scrutiny of Personnel's History Profile (PHP).
Performance concerns may result in appointment being withheld.

6.10 - RESERVES POLICY:
Requirements to enter Reserves:
- Reach Low Command in SAHP
- Have held a High Command position in subdivisions or a coordination spot
- No punishments within the last 4 weeks
- Have been active for the last 4 weeks
- Be in good standing with the HC team
- Has gone above and beyond for the department while continuing to show dedication

All Reserves are on 1SP (One Strike Policy) - Zero Tolerance Policy.

Reserve activity requirement: 4 hours a month (roughly 1 hour a week).
Reservists must abide by Uniform & Vehicle standards set forth in the master roster.
Reservists should NOT operate in a supervisory capacity whatsoever.
Reservists must NOT portray/represent themselves in a supervisor capacity either
(i.e., do NOT wear All Blue SAHP Command Uniform, drive vehicles with Polar Bear
Livery, or drive vehicles that say "Command" on them).

6.11 - INTERNAL AFFAIRS & PROMOTION AUDITOR:
Any LC member promoted to either Internal Affairs or Promotion Auditor will be placed
on a 30-day probationary period. One mistake = removal from the division.
(Both positions carry heavy responsibilities: IA for handling reports; Promotion
Auditor for handling skipped promotion tickets.)
The Commissioner has authority to prolong probationary time and handle removals.

6.12 - SAN ANDREAS HIGHWAY PATROL BRIEFINGS:
Captain+ (Low Command) may host in-game briefings with approval of the Highest
Command member on-duty.
Location: Highway Patrol Stations Briefing Room, postal 546.
Briefings may cover: mass patrol designated areas, enforcing rules and regulations,
addressing misconduct during patrols.

How to host a briefing (Captain and above):
1. Call it out in-game radio; tell people to stop what they're doing and head to
   Highway Patrol station, postal 546, Briefing Room.
2. Once there, put on formals and line up.
3. When everyone is present, begin covering the topic(s).
