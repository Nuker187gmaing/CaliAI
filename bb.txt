CALIFORNIA ROLEPLAY - BOOSTED BOIZ (BB)
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES
Written By: Ace Manhattan | 3/4/2023
================================================================================

PURPOSE AND SCOPE
================================================================================
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the guidelines and procedures
for members of Boosted Boiz, a street racing car group in CaliRP. The SOP
covers the rules and regulations for participating in street racing events and
activities organized by Boosted Boiz. The purpose is to ensure the safety of
all participants and to promote responsible behavior during events and activities.

================================================================================
SECTION 1 - SAFETY
================================================================================
1.1 Members of Boosted Boiz must prioritize safety during all events and
    activities. This includes following all traffic laws and regulations,
    wearing appropriate safety gear, and avoiding reckless driving.

1.2 Members must ensure their vehicles are in good condition and suitable for
    street racing. This includes regular maintenance and inspections, as well
    as complying with all applicable laws and regulations regarding vehicle
    modifications.

1.3 Members must avoid engaging in any behavior that could endanger the lives
    or safety of others, including pedestrians, other drivers, and passengers.

1.4 Members must not engage in any activity that is unethical, including the
    use of drugs or alcohol during events and activities.

1.5 Members must ensure they are physically and mentally fit to participate in
    street racing events and activities.

1.6 Members must follow the instructions of the group leader or an officer in
    charge regarding safety and emergency procedures.

1.7 Members must refrain from engaging in any activity that could lead to abuse
    of the vehicles provided, such as using them to VDM/RDM players or abusing
    speeds, etc.

1.8 Members must ensure they are wearing the head-tag in-game to drive the
    vehicles.

================================================================================
SECTION 2 - CONDUCT
================================================================================
2.1 Members of Boosted Boiz must conduct themselves in a professional and
    respectful manner at all times, including when interacting with other
    members, participants, and spectators.

2.2 Members must avoid engaging in any illegal or unethical behavior, including
    the use of drugs or alcohol during events and activities.

2.3 Members must follow the instructions of the group leader or an officer in
    charge and cooperate with other members and participants.

2.4 Members must not engage in any behavior that could damage the reputation of
    Boosted Boiz or other street racing car groups.

2.5 Members must respect the property and belongings of others, including
    participants and spectators.

2.6 Members must refrain from engaging in any activity that could lead to
    conflicts or confrontations with law enforcement officials or other
    authorities.

2.7 Members must not abuse the vehicles provided by using them to VDM/RDM
    players or abusing speeds, etc.

================================================================================
SECTION 3 - ILLEGAL ACTIVITIES
================================================================================
3.1 Members of Boosted Boiz are allowed to participate in illegal street races
    and evade law enforcement, but they must NOT engage in any violent or
    aggressive behavior towards law enforcement officials.

3.2 Members must follow all safety rules and regulations during illegal
    activities to ensure the safety of all participants.

3.3 Members must NOT shoot at law enforcement officials under any circumstances
    unless their life is in danger and there is a direct threat.

================================================================================
SECTION 4 - REPORTING INCIDENTS
================================================================================
4.1 Members involved in any accidents or incidents must report them immediately
    to the group leader or an officer in charge.

4.2 Members must provide a full and accurate account of the incident, including
    the circumstances that led to the accident or incident.

4.3 Members must not attempt to flee the scene of an accident or incident.

================================================================================
SECTION 5 - GUN RULES
================================================================================
4.4 Members must always follow the gun rules and restrictions. Failure to do so
    will result in TERMINATION or BLACKLIST.

4.5 Members may only use a firearm in a situation where they feel their life is
    threatened.

4.6 Using weapons on police is PROHIBITED.

4.7 While on as Boosted Boiz, members are only allowed to use their side
    firearm (pistol). The ONLY exception is if they are at a location and have
    brought a rifle/shotgun in their vehicle.

================================================================================
BACKGROUND STORY
================================================================================
Boosted Boiz is a group of passionate car enthusiasts who have come together to
form a tight-knit community in the heart of Los Angeles. They share a common
love for high-performance vehicles and the thrill of street racing, bonding over
shared experiences and the excitement of pushing the limits of automotive
engineering and speed.

The members of Boosted Boiz come from a variety of backgrounds, but all share a
deep respect and appreciation for the art of automotive design and the thrill of
driving. Many are skilled mechanics who have spent countless hours tweaking and
tuning their vehicles to achieve the perfect balance of power and speed. Others
are talented drivers who have honed their skills on the streets of Los Angeles.

Despite the dangers of illegal street racing, Boosted Boiz remains committed to
their passion for high-performance cars and adrenaline-fueled competition. They
have gained a reputation in the local street racing community for their
unwavering dedication to the sport and their willingness to take on even the
most formidable opponents.

For Boosted Boiz, street racing is more than just a hobby - it's a way of life.
Whether tuning engines in the garage, practicing driving on the streets, or
facing off in high-stakes competitions, the members of Boosted Boiz are united
by their shared passion and love of the ride.
