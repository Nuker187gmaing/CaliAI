CALIFORNIA ROLEPLAY - SAN ANDREAS TASK FORCE (SATF)
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)
Document Code: SOP-SATF-001 | Clearance Level: Department-Wide
Effective Date: April 11, 2026
================================================================================

1. PURPOSE
================================================================================
This SOP establishes the policies, structure, and operational standards for all
personnel within the San Andreas Task Force (SATF). All members are required to
understand and comply with this SOP at all times.

================================================================================
2. MISSION STATEMENT
================================================================================
The San Andreas Task Force (SATF) is a statewide law enforcement agency
responsible for:
- Major crime enforcement
- High-risk tactical operations
- Organized crime investigations
- Public safety and interagency support

"To protect the State of San Andreas through discipline, professionalism, and
teamwork."

================================================================================
3. JURISDICTION & AUTHORITY
================================================================================
- SATF operates statewide with full jurisdiction.
- May operate across all cities and counties.
- Works alongside other agencies when required.
- Must follow all server and roleplay regulations.

10. RADIO & DISPATCH POLICY
================================================================================

- Identify with callsign before speaking.
- Report: traffic stops, pursuits, and emergencies.

  Code 2 - Lights Only
  Code 3 - Lights & Sirens

  Code 5 - High Risk / Felony Stop

================================================================================
11. CAD POLICY
================================================================================
- All calls, reports, and actions must be logged.
- No false or misleading entries.
- Do not share CAD access.

================================================================================
12. USE OF FORCE POLICY
================================================================================
Force must always be:
- Necessary
- Reasonable
- Proportional

FORCE LEVELS:
  1. Officer Presence
  2. Verbal Commands
  3. Control Techniques
  4. Less-Lethal
  5. Deadly Force

================================================================================
13. DEADLY FORCE POLICY
================================================================================
Deadly force is authorized ONLY when ALL THREE conditions are met:
- Capability: Does the subject have the capability to harm you or others?
- Opportunity: Does the subject have the opportunity to harm you or others?
- Intent: Does the subject have the intent to harm you or others?

================================================================================
14. MIRANDA RIGHTS
================================================================================
Miranda must be read when a suspect is IN CUSTODY AND being questioned.

================================================================================
15. TACTICAL PROCEDURES
================================================================================

10-11 TRAFFIC STOP:
- Call out location and vehicle.
- Position safely.
- Approach with caution.
- Observe occupant behavior.

CODE 5 (FELONY STOP):
- Wait for backup.
- Draw weapons if necessary.
- One officer gives commands.
- Remove occupants one at a time.

10-80 VEHICLE PURSUIT:
- Call out direction, speed, and vehicle.
- Maintain safe distance.
- Follow supervisor instructions.

10-70 FOOT PURSUIT:
- Call out direction and description.
- Maintain visual contact.
- Request backup.

================================================================================
16. BASIC LIFE SUPPORT (BLS)
================================================================================
- Check injured individuals.
- Control bleeding.
- Stabilize condition.
- Request ESU / EMS.
