CALIRP BUSINESSES STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES
================================================================================

INTRODUCTION
================================================================================
This is the official California Roleplay Businesses Standard Operating Procedure.
This document includes all regulations and guidelines for CaliRP Businesses.
All topics under this document must be followed at all times.
All rules are subject to change at any time.

The CaliRP Business Coordination Team is here to make sure everything runs smoothly
and everyone feels welcome. We help members with applications, claiming locations,
and managing their businesses. Our goal is to improve the experience for business
owners and members by ensuring they follow the rules and helping them operate
effectively within the community.

================================================================================
JOINING A BUSINESS
================================================================================
All open business applications will be displayed on the Business Hub, located in
the Business Adverts channel. The Business Adverts channel is an interactive,
in-depth business advertisement that allows you to view all different categories
of businesses and read more about the mission of a business you wish to join.

You may be part of as many businesses as you wish, ONE security business and
ONE Car Club.

================================================================================
LOCATIONS
================================================================================
All Businesses & Car Clubs get 2 FREE locations to be used at the discretion of
the Business Command of that business.

Rules:
- A Business/Car Club CANNOT intrude, trespass, or claim a location already claimed
  by another business or gang. The subject may be removed from their business and
  arrested by local authorities.
- Any YMAP a business buys is automatically added to their location upon making a ticket.
- If you have claimed a postal, you must wait 30 days to claim another one using that slot.
- You are NOT permitted to claim a YMAP as a location not owned by your business.
- Interiors are NOT permitted to be claimed.

All locations must be approved by a member of the Boards of Directors. It is at
their discretion to approve or deny the location without a reason needed.

================================================================================
RESIGNATIONS & TRANSFERS
================================================================================
Businesses do NOT allow transfers between businesses. If you wish to join another
business, you must retire and join after serving your resignation cooldown.

Resignation cooldown: 3 days, unless approved by a member of the Boards of Directors.

================================================================================
DOCUMENTS
================================================================================
All businesses require working, updated documents that reflect the rules in this
SOP as well as their operating procedures. All employees must follow their
respective SOPs as well as this SOP. All Documents By Businesses must be Owned
By a Board of Directors member.

All Businesses must have the following:
- Standard Operating Procedures
- Roster
- Training Guide (optional, if a training/orientation is required)
- Google Form Application (optional)

================================================================================
GLOBAL BUSINESS GUIDELINES
================================================================================
As a business, you are required to follow global business regulations and CaliRP
Server Guidelines. Failure to follow the rules below can and will result in
disciplinary actions. Business Coordination and Business Command are responsible
for ensuring their employees follow these guidelines.

GENERAL RULES:
- You MUST always be in your Business's VC while on as that respective business.
- Never disrespect, threaten, or act toxic towards another member or business.
- You may be part of as many businesses as you wish: ONE security business and
  ONE Car Club.
- Businesses may declare themselves Passive, though both parties are NOT permitted
  to use violence in any way.
- All business members must have a registered character in CAD.
- You may break the law, though LEO may arrest employees who break the law.
- Uphold both this SOP and the California Roleplay Server Rules.
- A business may only have five HC members and five LC members (two may be THC ranks),
  unless Tier 2/Tier 3.
- Submit any documents created for the business to a Board of Directors member.
- An employee can only be promoted one rank at a time unless approved by SBC.
- Wait seven days after a promotion before being promoted again.
- Roleplay with the utmost respect and professionalism.
- Instatements may only be up to the highest non-supervisory rank; everything else
  must be approved by a LBC+.
- Businesses declared as passive CANNOT operate a security division; they must
  contract a security business if needed.
- Business Command CANNOT give out business tags to non-members, including in
  business partnerships.
- If a business member role-plays with another member, they must NOT poach, promote,
  discuss joining, or compare the businesses they are involved in.
- Pistols may be used at the discretion of High Command (excluding AP pistols/machine
  pistols).
- Declaring passive does NOT exempt you from LEO action if laws are broken. If the
  business breaks passive (e.g., engages in violence), they will be treated as
  the aggressor.
- You CANNOT declare passive on an area or scene that you do not own.
- You CANNOT declare an area or scene passive mid-way through.
- You may run from the police but CANNOT engage in violent activities (cannot be
  violent against LEO in any way or form).
- You cannot initiate violence; self defense is permitted (Fear RP must be followed).

YOU ARE NON-PASSIVE IF YOU DO ANY OF THE FOLLOWING:
- Break a law
- Declare yourselves non-passive
- Have a weapon
- Initiate or partake in violence

IF YOUR BUSINESS IS OPERATING AS SECURITY OR SIMILAR FUNCTION:
- All weapons must be legally registered in CAD.
- LEO instructions must be followed at all times.
- If detaining a suspect, you MUST call LEOs; you CANNOT jail them yourself.

BUSINESS RECRUITMENT GUIDELINES
================================================================================
There are two types of Recruitment: Roleplay and Non-Roleplay.

ROLEPLAY RECRUITMENT:
Businesses may allow certain ranks to hire civilians during in-server roleplay
scenarios (e.g., during car meets or when a civilian enters their store requesting
a job). Business Command must ensure the business SOP is strictly followed.

NON-ROLEPLAY RECRUITMENT:
Recruitments can be held at popular locations such as Sandy Shores, Paleto Bay,
and Legion Square.

When hosting a recruitment, the following rules apply:
- 1 car per recruitment
- Up to 3 props are permitted (reduce if disrupting the surrounding area)
- No limit on the amount of recruiters

Businesses must remain professional at all times. If someone disrupts a recruitment,
call staff via the /calladmin command and be patient.
These recruitments are hosted outside of character.

================================================================================
IDENTIFIERS
================================================================================
- When on duty, you MUST have your Business's headtag/gangtag enabled.
- Your name must always include the abbreviation of your business in the Business Discord.
  Example: G6 | M. Max
- Your name must be professional and realistic.
- You may have a "!" in front of your name if you're a Business Coordinator or
  Business High Command.
- Business Low Command may use a "#".

================================================================================
BUSINESS COMMAND
================================================================================
- The Business Command role is given to ranks (00)01-05 of each Business.
- The Business Owner role is given to the highest-ranking member of each Business.
- You may only be in command of ONE Business at a time.
- Business Command is prohibited from making decisions based on biased opinions
  or personal relationships.

COMMAND STRUCTURE LIMITS:
- 5 HC and 5 LC positions maximum (with 2 THC positions: 5 HC, 2 THC, 3 LC),
  unless Tier 2 (grants 5 additional LC/THC slots) or Tier 3 (another 5 for LC/THC).
- Any movement from Low Command to High Command requires approval from SBC and above,
  including internal movements within the High Command team.
- The Liaison of that business is to be contacted for HC promotions/movements.
- Only ONE person may fill each rank within the command team.
  Maximum of 10 command members unless there is an overseer.

================================================================================
CONTRACTS & PARTNERSHIPS
================================================================================
All Business Ownership/Command may contract or partner with all legal parties,
including LEO, DOD, Security Businesses, etc.

Rules:
- All contracts and partnerships must be discussed with both parties' HC and
  approved by Business Coordinators.
- Both Business High Command Teams must ensure the contract is not breached at
  any time.
- Contracts with Gangs are permitted, though you may be arrested or face legal
  consequences in roleplay.

================================================================================
MERGES & TRANSFERS
================================================================================
- A merge may only be approved by a member of the Board of Directors.
- Merges are only permitted within the same section of the business (e.g., a
  security company CANNOT merge with a car club).

RANK POLICY FOR TRANSFERS/MERGES:
  High Command          -> Lowest Low Command / Highest Supervisor
  THC & LC              -> Highest Supervisor
  Supervisors & Employees -> 1 rank demotion
  Lowest Rank (after probationary rank) -> Lateral unless stated otherwise

NOTE: Transferred individuals are on a 7-day promotion cooldown.
Lead Business Coordinators+ have discretion regarding rank for transfers/merges.
