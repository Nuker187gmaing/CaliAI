SECTION 1 | INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

1.01 | CARTEL EXPECTATIONS
- You MUST comply with both the CaliRP Gang Community SOP and the CaliRP Server Rules.
- You may belong to ONLY ONE cartel at a time.
- All roleplay must meet the highest possible standard — poor roleplay quality will
  result in consequences.
- If a member BELOW High Command leaves a cartel, they must serve a 3-day parole.
- HIGH COMMAND members must serve a 5-day parole upon leaving.
- Punishments follow the CaliRP Gang Punishment Guidelines V3 and may be updated
  at any time.
- Gun rules are mandatory — violations are punished by Cartel Coordinators.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.02 | CARTEL COORDINATION
Cartel Coordinators play an essential role in maintaining the structure, quality,
and integrity of cartel roleplay. Their responsibilities:
- Oversee all cartel-related activity to ensure consistency, creativity, and
  adherence to server standards.
- Stay active within the roleplay scene; support cartel members and HC/LC.
- Help create engaging roleplay that strengthens the overall community experience.
- Act as a bridge between individual cartels and Cartel Heads.
- Assist with administrative tasks; help interpret and enforce community guidelines.
- Step in to resolve issues or conflicts before they escalate.
- Revoke or reinstate unjust punishments or removals (per Cartel Coordination
  Structure).
- Provide guidance, resources, and structure to help cartels grow and deliver
  high-quality roleplay.

SECTION 2 | CARTEL GUIDELINES
================================================================================

2.01 | OUTFIT REGULATIONS
Uniforms should be as realistic as possible. Cartels use certain colors due to EUP
and style requirements.

"SHITTER FITS" are STRICTLY PROHIBITED and will result in punishment.

TACTICAL / CARTEL OUTFIT POLICY:
Purpose: Ensure outfits are realistic, safe, and appropriate for roleplay scenes.
Prevent misuse of heavy tactical gear in low-risk/public settings.

WHEN ALLOWED:
- Full tactical gear (rifles, helmets, NVGs, heavy plate, full-face goggles) is
  permitted ONLY during raids, active combat ops, or explicitly designated
  high-risk scenarios.

WHEN PROHIBITED:
- Do NOT wear full tactical loadouts in passive/public business or street scenes
  (e.g., casual street RP, shops, offices).

PERMITTED EVERYDAY KIT:
- Low-profile items (hoodies, jeans, concealable/soft armor, simple vests) are
  acceptable for non-raid RP.

APPEARANCE GUIDELINE:
- Keep uniforms realistic — no bright, attention-grabbing color schemes or
  mismatched costume elements that make the outfit obviously theatrical or
  out-of-place.
- Remake any outfits that look unrealistic or overly conspicuous for everyday
  city RP.

ENFORCEMENT:
- Repeated violations (unwarranted heavy gear, blatant attention-seeking outfits,
  frequent inappropriate raid initiation) will result in warnings and possible
  demotion from tiers.

SUMMARY: Heavy/tactical = raid-only. Low-profile = acceptable for everyday
cartel/militia RP. Keep it realistic and situational.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.02 | FEAR ROLEPLAY / RAIDS AND LIFE RULE

FEAR HIERARCHY:
- Tier 3 Cartels do NOT need to fear anyone else in their category.
- Tier 2s MUST fear a Tier 3 if outnumbered.
- Tier 1s MUST fear Tier 2 AND Tier 3 at all times if outnumbered.

RAIDS:
- By default, all raids are BOG (Boots On Ground).
- NO weaponized vehicles are permitted at any point unless agreed upon by BOTH
  gangs prior to the raid.

LIFE RULE:
- 1 life per scene at all times with Gangs & LEO, unless it is a planned raid and
  both sides agree on more lives.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.03 | RANK STRUCTURE / SUBDIVISION RULES

RANK STRUCTURE:
  Overseers:     00 (Earned or Purchased)
  High Command:  01 - 05
  Low Command:   06 - 010

RANK SLOT RULES:
- Each command rank can only have ONE member per slot (e.g., only one 05, one 010).
- Exception: If your group has purchased double rank slots, up to TWO members are
  allowed per rank (e.g., two 01s or two 04s).
- Multiple Overseers (00s) are allowed based on the number of slots your group owns.
- Any group found with unauthorized duplicate command ranks will receive IMMEDIATE
  punishment without warning.

SUBDIVISIONS:
- Any new subdivision must first be APPROVED before creation.
- Open a creation ticket for review to ensure the subdivision name and concept
  align with current community guidelines.

SECTION 3 | CARTEL INTERACTIONS
================================================================================

3.01 | GENERAL CARTEL INTERACTIONS

LIFE RULE:
- Each member gets ONE life per scene unless a different rule is clearly stated in
  a /gme before the scene starts.
- The group that INITIATES the scene sets the life rule; it CANNOT be changed
  mid-scene.

PROPERTY RULE:
- If your cartel owns a postal or YMAP, you can request another group to leave OOC
  by using /calladmin or contacting Cartel Coordination.
- This should ONLY be done if the scene gets out of control — NOT to avoid roleplay.

IN-CHARACTER BEHAVIOR:
- Stay in character at all times when roleplaying as or with a cartel.
- Going OOC or disrupting scenes will result in punishment.

QUALITY ROLEPLAY:
- Every scene must have roleplay build-up. Random or unprovoked attacks are NOT
  allowed.
- "At war" is NOT an excuse for Kill On Sight (KOS) behavior.
- Repeated toxic or random attacks will lead to group strikes or punishments.

INTERACTION RULES:
- New Life Rule: 1 life per scene — you have NO memory of your past life after dying.
- Stay In Character: No OOC talk during roleplay.
- Fear RP: Always show proper fear in realistic situations.
- Gang Tag: You MUST wear the correct group tag (e.g., Madrazo Cartel tag when on
  that character).
- Cartel vs. Street Gangs: Cartels can ONLY attack street gangs if provoked first.
  Retaliation must happen within the same scene. Street gangs must fear cartels —
  report any abuse of this rule to the Cartel/Street Gang Coordinator with proof.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.02 | LEO INTERACTIONS

TRAFFIC STOPS:
- If cooldown or priority is not active, you MAY run from LEOs — but don't do it
  every time. Always aim to create good, realistic RP for both sides.
- If you stop, provide your CAD info and roleplay the scene properly.

LEO RAIDS:
- You may defend yourself during a raid, especially on your own property, but
  follow life rules.
- LEOs get 2 lives; you get 1, unless the owning gang's /gme states otherwise.
- Always fear for your freedom — LEOs can arrest you for life, opening future RP
  like warrants or BOLOs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.03 | CARTEL BEEF STAGES
Cartels must progress through beef stages in order. Jumping stages is NOT permitted.

STAGE 1:
  - Violent aggression
  - Theft of assets (outside of gang properties)
  - Use of weapons (within gun rules)
  - Raiding properties (5 minutes prior RP required)
  - All raids are boots on ground
  - Allies CANNOT be involved
  - Must remain in Stage 1 for 1 week

STAGE 2:
  - Violent aggression
  - Use of weapons (within gun rules)
  - Theft of assets (within gang properties)
  - Raiding all properties (with GME)
  - Allies CANNOT be involved
  - Boots-on-ground subject to agreement by both parties
  - Must remain in Stage 2 for 1 week

STAGE 3:
  - Free locations can be put up for grabs at turf wars (must be agreed by both)
  - All-out war
  - Boots-on-ground subject to agreement by both parties
  - KOS on sight (if marked)
  - Allies CAN be involved
