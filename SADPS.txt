5 | Miranda Rights 
(Read to individuals during questioning).

"You have the right to remain silent. Anything you say can and will be used against you in the court of law. You have the right to an attorney, if you cannot afford an attorney one will be appointed to you by the State of California. 

Do you understand these rights that I have read to you? With these rights in mind, do you wish to speak to me further?"

When Are Miranda Rights Required?
Miranda rights must be read when two conditions are met:
1. Custody: The individual is not free to leave, meaning they are under arrest or their freedom is significantly restricted.
2. Interrogation: The individual is being questioned in a way that is likely to elicit incriminating responses.
6 | Punishment Guidelines
The following structure outlines the process for administering internal corrective actions for members of the Department of Public Safety (DPS). While punishments may be escalated in extreme cases, this framework serves as the standard procedure for maintaining accountability and professionalism within the department.

1. Oral Reprimand
2. Written Reprimand

4. Demotion

6.2 | Additional Guidelines:
   * Members may be terminated if they breach rules or guidelines in a manner consistent with these corrective action steps.
High Command or Low Command may issue a department blacklist to terminated members at their discretion.

   * Termination will trigger a resignation cooldown period, during which the member cannot rejoin DPS.

   * Any member banned or blacklisted from the server or community for more than two weeks will be automatically terminated from DPS. Reinstatement is solely at the discretion of High Command.

7 | Radio Traffic Only (RTO) Etiquette.
      * When Dispatch is active in RTO, you must send all traffic through them by first requesting        permission to speak (“ Callsign to Dispatch ”). Once cleared to transmit, you will provide them with the necessary information related to your current situation. Dispatch is responsible for priority response coordination and you are required to abide by their directions.
      * If no Dispatcher is active, you are permitted to Self-Dispatch but must adhere to the RTO authority listed below.
      * If you need to or wish to speak with another officer, you must request a  10-1 and specify which channel in the process (e.g. “<Callsign>, to <Callsign> direct, can you 10-1 to channel 1”). Example with our callsign, “This is DPS-01 to DPS-25, please join channel 10-1 channel 2”
      * You must call-out if you are going 10-7 (Out of service) or 10-6 (Busy/ INOP), changing it on the CAD system is not good enough. All other statuses, for example, setting yourself 10-8 after a call, can be set solely in the CAD system and may be accompanied by a call-out in the RTO channel.

      * If you need to call through a 10-11 (Traffic stop) You MUST ask dispatch beforehand if he is ready for the info, failing to do this will result in an RTO strike.

      * FailRP/VDM/RDM is to not be called out in RTO . It is at your discretion to disregard calls which seem unreasonable.

      * Only communicate in RTO when realistic (I.e. Don’t shout that you are dead, if you are dead you do not have the capability to speak through your radio).

8.2 | Uniforms:
All SADPS must use rank appropriate uniforms, this means you must use uniforms that are provided by HC for your current rank. 
All uniforms that are custom made like UC and etc. need to be approved by HC and must stay professional. 
Formal uniforms must be worn while in RTO briefings. 

8.3 | Vehicles:
        The uniforms can be found in SADPS Roster.

      * Officers may use vehicles that are under your current rank. 
      * Units are only allowed to go a maximum of 180 mph in an active car pursuit, only units that are heat certified are allowed to go 225 mph in a car pursuit.
      * All vehicles must be locked at all times.
      * Driving against oncoming traffic to pursue a suspect during a 10-80 is prohibited. Officers must exercise due regard for public safety and follow all pursuit protocols to minimize risks during pursuits.
      * All LEOs are expected to follow all standard rules and regulations of California Traffic Law when not responding to a priority. During priorities, LEO is expected to maintain control of their vehicle and prioritize the safety of other drivers.
      * Our department license plate format is XXDPSXXX (The first 2 numbers are your choice of numbers and the 3 last numbers must be your badge numbers). 
      * The vehicles that are locked to a subdivision only are allowed to be use whenever you are on as that subdivision.
      * You are not permitted to use both the visor lights & lightbar on your vehicle. Only supervisors are permitted to be using visor lights and regular patrol officers/deputies should be using the lightbar attachment. If the vehicle is not supplied with a lightbar extra you are exempt from this requirement.        

9 | Activity Requirements
      * In the Department of Public Safety (DPS), all officers are required to meet a weekly activity requirement of 3 hours per week to be considered active. 
      * If an officer is unable to complete the 3-hour requirement, they may:  
      * - Request a Leave of Absence (LOA)
      * - Be placed in Reserves
      * For more information, please refer to the LOA and Reserves Policies sections.
        
10 | Resignation, Reserves, Leave Of Absence (LOA) Policies.
10.1 | Resignation
      * If an officer wishes to resign from his current position he may do that by creating a resignation ticket in the SADPS discord chanell tickets, when this occurs the member will be placed under a 1 week LEO cooldown.
      * The one week cooldown only applies for the type of department the person has resigned from, i.e.-if you resign from SADPS, you can not join BCSO for one week, however, you can join a Pre-Main department right away since they are able to be in both departments at the same time anyways.
      * Once someone resigns from a department (not including transfers) they may only be reinstated within a 30 day period, if the 30 day period has passed they must be retrained as a cadet within the department.
10.2 | Reserves
      * If you wish to stay in the department and don’t want to resign but cannot keep up the required weekly hours you may enter our department reserves.
      * Reserves are for those people that cannot keep up weekly hours, when ever you are on as reserves you are rank locked so you may not get a promotion while being on as reserves, but you will only need to do 5 hours a month.
      * If you decide to come out of reserve status after 60 days of being in reserve status, you will need to be placed at Constable rank
      * If, within 30 days of being placed on reserve status, someone below Low Command chooses to come off reserve, they may return to their position with a one-rank demotion.
      * Low Command ranks are equivalent to the highest Supervisor level, while THC+ ranks are considered the lowest level within Low Command.
      * TID resets with reserve status; members need 60 days in-department post-reserve for transfer eligibility.

10.3 Leave Of Absence (LOA).
      * If a person wants to leave for some time, he may request LOA or Leave Of Absence in SADPS discord channel named LOA Request.
      * You may request LOA at any time you would like but the maximum allowed LOA time is 60 days. You may not request more than that.

11 | DPS Subdivisions
The Department of Public Safety (DPS) offers a variety of specialized subdivisions for members to serve in, each focusing on distinct areas of law enforcement and public safety. These units provide opportunities for officers to develop expertise, take on unique responsibilities, and contribute to the overall mission of the department:
      * Gang Intelligence Unit (GIU) – Responsible for monitoring, investigating, and dismantling gang-related activities within the city.

      * Internal Affairs (IA) – Ensures department integrity by investigating internal misconduct and upholding professional standards.

      * Motorcycle Unit (Motor Unit) – Specializes in traffic enforcement, rapid response, and community engagement on two wheels.

      * Training & Recruitment Division – Manages the recruitment, onboarding, and ongoing training of DPS personnel to maintain a highly skilled force.

      * Special Emergency Response Team (SERT) – Handles high-risk operations, critical incidents, and emergency situations requiring specialized tactics.

San Andreas Department of Public Safety
Promotional Guidelines
________________

This document establishes the promotion guidelines and activity requirements for all ranks within the San Andreas Department of Public Safety. Its purpose is to maintain departmental structure, ensure consistent expectations across all ranks, and provide a fair and unbiased framework for promotional decisions. These requirements exist to support accountability, professionalism, and sustained activity throughout the department.

All ranks within the San Andreas Department of Public Safety are required to meet the outlined promotional and activity standards in order to maintain their current rank and status within the department.

All members must satisfy the requirements associated with their current rank before being considered for promotion. Promotion eligibility is reviewed on a weekly basis for all ranks and is strictly merit-based. Meeting minimum activity requirements does not guarantee promotion.

The San Andreas Department of Public Safety High Command retains full authority to approve, delay, or deny any promotion at their discretion. This authority exists to ensure that promotions reflect departmental needs, professional conduct, performance, and leadership capability.

Any member who has received a field promotion is still required to complete all unmet requirements from previous ranks. Failure to complete those requirements will result in ineligibility for further promotion until all prior obligations have been fulfilled.

Cadet
	Rank Requirement
	Complete DPS basic training within 2 weeks.
	Pass your training.
	

Probationary Constable
	Promotion Requirements
	Serve at least 1 week in rank.
	Pass your Ridealong.
	Clock at least 3 hours within the week.
	No active disciplinary actions in the last 7 days.
	

Constable
	Promotion Requirements
	Serve at least 1 week in rank.
	Clock at least 4 hours within the week (patrol + other duties).
	Be active in at least 1 subdivision.
	Earn Recruitment Badge (submit application to RTD).
	No active disciplinary actions in the last 7 days.
	

Senior Constable
	Promotion Requirements
	Serve at least 1 week in rank.
	Clock at least 5 hours within the week (patrol + other duties).
	Be active in at least 1 subdivision.
	Host 2 Open Recruitments.
	Obtain FTA Badge (FTA exam required).
	No active disciplinary actions in the last 7 days.
	

Corporal
	Promotion Requirements
	Serve at least 1 week in rank.
	Clock at least 5 hours within the week (patrol + other duties).
	Be active in at least 1 subdivision.
	Assist with 1 Basic Training session.
	Host 1 Ridealong.
	Pass Sergeant Exam to qualify for promotion.
	No active disciplinary actions in the last 7 days.
	

Sergeant
	Promotion Requirements
	Serve at least 1 week in rank.
	Clock at least 5 hours within the week (patrol + other duties).
	Be active in at least 1 subdivision.
	Assist with 1 Basic Training session.
	Host 1 Ridealong.
	Host 2 Open Recruitments.
	Obtain FTO Badge (FTO exam required).
	No active disciplinary actions in the last 7 days.
	

Staff Sergeant
	Promotion Requirements
	Serve at least 1 week in rank.
	Clock at least 5 hours within the week (patrol + other duties).
	Be active in at least 1 subdivision.
	Host 1 Basic Training session.
	Host 1 Ridealong.
	Host 2 Open Recruitments.
	No active disciplinary actions in the last 7 days.
	

Master Sergeant
	Promotion Requirements
	Serve at least 1 week in rank.
	Clock at least 5 hours within the week (made of patrol and the below)
	Be active in at least 2 subdivisions.
	Host 1 Basic Training
	Host 2 Open Recruitments
	Pass Lieutenant Exam.
	No active disciplinary actions in the last 7 days.
	

Second Lieutenant
	Promotion Requirements
	Serve at least 1 week in rank.
	Clock at least 5 hours within the week (made of patrol and the below)
	Be active in at least 2 subdivisions.
	Host 1 Basic Training session.
	Recruit 2 new members into DPS.

	No disciplinary actions in the last 4 weeks.
	No active disciplinary actions in the last 7 days.

Low Command

	Serve at least 2 weeks in rank.
	Clock at least 7 hours A Week (made of patrol and the below)

	Recruit 2 new members into DPS.
	Host & grade 1 Sergeant or Lieutenant Exam.
	Get 2 hours / be active for subdivisions
	Hold a minimum of 1 coordination position
	Do task assigned by THC+
	No active disciplinary actions in the last 7 days.
	Promotion is handpicked by High Command.
