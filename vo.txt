CALIFORNIA ROLEPLAY
VOLUNTEER OFFICER (VO) STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES
================================================================================

INTRODUCTION
================================================================================
Welcome to the California Roleplay Community. This document covers the basics and
standards for being a Volunteer Officer in CaliRP. All guidelines outlined here
are to be followed at all times by all Volunteer Officers while on duty.

Failure to follow these guidelines could result in being sent off duty by a member
of the Department of Homefront Security (DOHS).

REQUIRED READING BEFORE GOING ON DUTY:
CaliRP | Global Departments SOP 2024

CaliRP Owners: 101 | Ren, 102 | Ryan S., 103 | J. Serg

================================================================================
SECTION 1 | GENERAL INFORMATION FOR VOs
================================================================================

1.1 | VOLUNTEER DEPARTMENT PURPOSE
The Volunteer Police Department is intended for new members of the California
Roleplay community to:
- Familiarize themselves with the server's policies, rules, and LEO perks.
- Decide if joining a department and working up the ranks is for them.

Inside the department, several Main Departments, Pre-Main Departments, and DOD
departments can recruit new members.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.2 | UNDERSTANDING DOHS
The Department of Homefront Security (DOHS) is responsible for overseeing all LEO
departments and ensuring those departments are GSOP compliant. This includes
Volunteer Officers.

When requesting DOHS assistance, you MUST follow the chain of command.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.3 | CHAIN OF COMMAND

  Overseers                    - P. Cotton, J. Rice
  Department Coordination Team
  VO Coordinators
  DOHS Command / Supervisors   - Callsigns 100-300
  DOHS Agents                  - Callsigns 400-600

SECTION 2 | PROPER CAD CHECKS
================================================================================

2.1 | GETTING ON DUTY
Two steps to go on duty as a VO:
1. Do /duty with a proper roleplay name and VO callsign.
   Example formats: VO-460 | John R.  OR  VO-174 | J. Rice
2. Ensure your callsign and duty blip are correctly set.

Reference the tutorial channel in the Volunteer Officer Discord for step-by-step
instructions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2 | CAD SYSTEM
You MUST familiarize yourself with the CAD system.

IMPORTANT: Create TWO characters:
- Civilian Character
- LEO Character

Do NOT get arrested/charged on your LEO character — this may result in removal.

CAD: https://cad.calirp.gg/
Follow the CAD Tutorial to learn how to create your LEO and CIV character.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3 | NAME, CALLSIGN, AND LICENSE PLATE POLICIES

NAME STRUCTURE:
All Volunteer Officers must use one of the following naming conventions:
  - John R.
  - J. Rice

CALLSIGN STRUCTURE:
Format: VO-XXX (range: VO-101 to VO-999)
NOTE: VO-000 through VO-100 are RESERVED for Management.

- Callsigns can be any number of your choosing as long as they are professional.
- Do NOT use unprofessional numbers such as 420, 69, 666, etc.
- You are REQUIRED to give your callsign and name when asked by anyone while on duty.
  Failure to do so results in heavy consequences.
- When asked for your information, use /showbadge <id> or the third eye (Right Alt)
  and hit showbadge. No one is exempt from this rule (including DOHS, IAA, State
  Patrol). Civilians must accept via /viewbadge <id>.

LICENSE PLATE STRUCTURE:
Format: Two numbers + "CVO" + Three numbers
Example: 23CVO994
("CVO" stands for "Community Volunteer Officer")

- Plate numbers must be your callsign.
- Do NOT use unprofessional numbers (420, 69, 666, etc.).
- If the plate is taken in CAD, choose another number variation and update your
  callsign to match.

SECTION 3 | WEAPON, VEHICLE AND UNIFORM
================================================================================

3.1 | SERVICE WEAPON STRUCTURE
Authorized weapons for Volunteer Officers:
  - Pistol
  - Flashlight

NOTE: As a non-whitelisted LEO, you do NOT have access to:
  - Taser
  - Combat Pistol
  - Baton

Contact VO Coordinators if you have questions about weapons you have purchased.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 | SERVICE VEHICLE STRUCTURE
All Volunteer Officers are REQUIRED to drive the VO Charger and ONLY the VO Charger.
- Spawn Code: VOCHARGER
- You are permitted to drive personals you have access to and car pack vehicles.
- You CANNOT use the base LEO legacy vehicles.

LICENSE PLATE FORMAT (vehicle):
- First 2 numbers: any professional numbers of your choosing
  (no unprofessional numbers: 420, 69, 666, etc.)
- Last 3 numbers: anything besides unprofessional numbers
- If plate is taken in CAD, choose another number variation.

Contact VO Coordinators if you have questions about vehicles you have purchased.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 | UNIFORM STRUCTURE
Male On-Duty Uniform:    Outfit ID: 9621
Female On-Duty Uniform:  Outfit ID: 9623

================================================================================
SECTION 4 | PUNISHMENT GUIDELINES
================================================================================

4.1 | ABUSE OF PERMISSIONS
Being a Volunteer Officer grants unique perks such as the ability to do /duty and
unlock certain doors. Abuse of these permissions can result in:
- Removal from VO
- A possible blacklist

Use your permissions wisely and only to better your roleplay.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 | DOHS VIOLATIONS
DOHS's goal is to help push Volunteer Officers to join departments and make their
experience as a police officer enjoyable. If they pull you over, they are simply
trying to teach or better you.

- You MUST pull over for DOHS.
- Failure to do so can lead to being sent off duty or in-game punishment.

If you do not like how a DOHS or any other member is treating you, you may make a
report in the California Roleplay Discord using: /Dept Ticket Report

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 | PENAL CODES (FUTURE LEO REFERENCE)
When on as a Volunteer Officer, you do NOT have jailing permissions. It is
important to learn and know the Penal Codes for future reference as a LEO.

Reference: CaliRP | Penal Codes and Speed Limits 2024
