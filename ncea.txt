2. MISSION STATEMENT
================================================================================
The National Criminal Enforcement Agency (NCEA) is an elite, multi-agency unit
operating under the authority of the State Governor. Its mission is to combat
major crimes - including terrorism, organized crime, narcotics trafficking,
kidnappings, and murder-for-hire operations - through specialized tactics,
interagency cooperation, and advanced investigative methods.

5. DEPARTMENT MANDATE
================================================================================
NCEA is a classified multinational entity tasked with:
- Investigation: Surveillance, infiltration, and analysis of anomalous events,
  entities, and technologies.
- Containment: Secure isolation or neutralization of threats that fall outside
  conventional scientific or military understanding.
- Enforcement: Execution of field operations to eliminate, extract, or suppress
  anomalies and associated threats.

================================================================================
6. ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE
================================================================================
- Directorate Alpha (HQ Command): Strategic command, mission authorization,
  international liaison.
- Division I (Investigation & Analysis): Data collection, decoding, cyber-ops,
  HUMINT/SIGINT.
- Division C (Containment & Logistics): Engineering, research, site
  construction, anomaly storage.
- Division E (Enforcement & Tactical): Rapid-response field officers, special
  operations teams (NCEA-Units), high-risk engagement protocols.

8. MISSION PHASES & OPERATIONS
================================================================================

8.1 PRE-MISSION PROTOCOL:
- All missions require Directorate Alpha approval.
- A designated Mission Handler will brief all assigned NCEA-Units.
- Threat classification must be confirmed (see Section 9).
- Tactical loadout authorization and mental conditioning checks are mandatory.

8.2 DEPLOYMENT:
The NCEA GOST is activated for:
- Active terrorist threats or bombings
- High-profile kidnappings
- Organized crime takedowns (cartels, mafias, syndicates)
- Homicides involving multiple victims or public figures
- High-stakes undercover operations
- Hostage situations beyond standard LEO capability

8.3 ENGAGEMENT RULES:
- Non-lethal force preferred for human subjects unless threat is Code Red.
- Use of experimental tech or anomalous assets must be logged and approved prior
  to use.
- All encounters with entities must be recorded and reported within 2 hours
  post-operation.

8.4 CONTAINMENT PROTOCOL:
- Anomalies must be secured in approved Containment Transport Units (CTUs).
- Temporary field containment permitted for up to 12 hours pending extraction.
- Biological anomalies require quarantine and full biohazard lockdown.

8.5 DEBRIEF & REVIEW:
- Officers must undergo post-mission psychological evaluation within 24 hours.
- Full after-action reports (AARs) to be submitted in NCEA-Net v7.
- Any unauthorized deviation will trigger an internal review.

================================================================================
9. THREAT CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM
================================================================================
  Green  - Known anomaly, low risk, stable
  Amber  - Partially understood anomaly, moderate risk
  Red    - Unstable anomaly or entity with hostile capability
  Black  - Catastrophic potential (reality-altering, transdimensional)
  White  - Non-hostile or deactivated, requires ongoing observation

================================================================================
10. EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS
================================================================================
- Omega Lockdown: Activated upon breach of a Code Black entity or site-wide
  contamination.
- Clean Sweep: Tactical strike to erase evidence and neutralize exposed
  civilians.
- Protocol Null: Destruction of anomaly and termination of associated field
  assets in case of global risk.
- Data Purge: Immediate remote erasure of NCEA-Net systems under hostile
  compromise.

15. USE OF FORCE POLICY
================================================================================

15.1 PURPOSE:
Establishes rules of engagement for NCEA field personnel when interacting with
hostile entities, human subjects, or unknown anomalies.

15.2 PRINCIPLES OF ENGAGEMENT:
- Necessity: Force is to be used only when no lesser means are sufficient.
- Proportionality: The level of force must match the threat level posed.
- Discretion: Secrecy and deniability take priority over brute force unless
  lives are in immediate danger.
- Preservation: When possible, preserve life and evidence over termination.

15.3 FORCE CLASSIFICATIONS:
  Level 0 - Non-Force:       Verbal commands, psychological compliance,
                              passive restraints. Used for civilians or
                              cooperative individuals.
  Level 1 - Compliance:      Non-lethal takedowns: tasers, shock restraints,
                              sedatives, pressure-point maneuvers. Used for
                              unarmed resistance.
  Level 2 - Defensive Force: Baton strikes, combat holds, sidearm warnings.
                              Authorized when officers face physical threats.
  Level 3 - Lethal Force:    Direct use of firearms. Authorized in defense of
                              officer's life or when threat is classified Red.
  Level 4 - Anomalous Force: Deployment of sanctioned experimental tools.
                              Requires pre-approval from Directorate Alpha
                              unless in emergency.
  Level 5 - Protocol Null:   Full termination and erasure. Used only under
                              Protocol Null authorization for existential
                              threats. Requires confirmation from Command and
                              Ethics Liaison.

17. ACTIVITY REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

17.1 MINIMUM ACTIVITY:
- Officers must maintain a minimum of 5 hours per week active duty in-server.
- Participation in at least one major operation or training exercise per month
  is mandatory.
- Attendance at weekly briefings is required unless excused by command.
- 1st LT and Command are required to clock 40 hours per month.
- Sergeant+ need 3 recruitment logs for promotions and 1 recruitment per week
  for activity.

17.2 TRAINING:
- Completion of basic tactical training is mandatory for all new personnel.
- Quarterly certifications in tactical readiness and psychological resilience
  are required.
- Specialized trainings may be required based on assignment (Containment,
  Tactical, etc.).

================================================================================
18. LEAVE OF ABSENCE (LOA) POLICIES
================================================================================

18.1 APPLICATION:
- Personnel must submit an LOA request to their immediate supervisor via
  official communication channels (Discord).
- Requests must include reason, start date, and expected return date.

18.2 DURATION & APPROVAL:
- LOAs of up to 14 days are generally approved without extended review.
- LOAs longer than 14 days require Assistant Director approval.
- Emergency LOAs may be granted with verbal notification, followed by formal
  request.

18.3 RETURN:
- Personnel must notify command 24 hours prior to return.
- Failure to return without notice may result in disciplinary action.

================================================================================
19. PUNISHMENT GUIDELINES
================================================================================

19.1 STRIKE SYSTEM:
  First Offense:  Verbal warning and documentation.
  Second Offense: Written warning and mandatory retraining.
  Third Offense:  Suspension from duty for 3 to 7 days.
  Fourth Offense: Review for termination.
  NOTE: Command is on a ONE STRIKE POLICY.

19.2 SUSPENSION POLICIES:
- Suspension requires written notice from command detailing reason and duration.
- Suspended personnel are prohibited from performing any department-related
  duties.

19.3 TERMINATION & DISCHARGE:
- Termination decisions are made by the Director or Deputy Director after
  review.
- Grounds for termination include: gross misconduct, repeated violations,
  insubordination, or breach of operational secrecy.
- Discharge documentation is maintained in personnel records.

20. SUBDIVISIONS
================================================================================

20.1 DIVISION ALPHA - Headquarters Command & Strategy:
  Eligibility: Senior command officers only. Responsible for operational
  oversight.

20.2 R.I.P. - Rapid Intervention & Pursuit:
  Focus: High-Speed Interdiction & All-Terrain Enforcement.
  Role: Masters of the chase. Handles all high-speed pursuits on city highways
  and manages off-road apprehension in rural or mountainous terrain.

20.3 G.O.S.T. - Gang Operations & Surveillance Team:
  Focus: Undercover Operations & Deep-Cover Intelligence.
  Role: The "Ghost" unit. Operates in the shadows, focusing on long-term
  surveillance, infiltration of syndicates, and gathering RICO evidence to
  dismantle criminal organizations from the inside out.

20.4 V.I.P.E.R. - Violent Intervention & Pursuit Engagement Response:
  Focus: Elite Tactical Operations & Heavy Enforcement.
  Role: The agency's premier tactical unit. Deployed for high-risk warrants,
  active shooters, and coordinated raids.

20.5 C.O.R.T. - Critical Operations Rescue Team:
  Eligibility: Must be a trained officer. Requires Advanced Narcotics
  Certification.

20.6 M.A.P.S. - Marine & Air Patrol Section:
  Focus: Tactical Medicine & Medivac Operations.
  Role: The agency's elite medical wing. Provides tactical EMT support during
  V.I.P.E.R. raids and operates Medivac helicopters for rapid life-saving
  extractions in high-threat environments.

20.7 K9 - Canine Division:
  Eligibility: Must be a trained officer. Requires Advanced Narcotics
  Certification.

25. USE OF FORCE PYRAMID (EXPANDED)
================================================================================
  Level 0 - Verbal Commands:  De-escalation, clear instructions.
  Level 1 - Compliance Force: Control holds, taser, non-lethal methods.
  Level 2 - Defensive Force:  Baton strikes, pepper spray, warning shots.
  Level 3 - Lethal Force:     Use of firearms under justified conditions.
  Level 4 - Anomalous Force:  Use of sanctioned anomalous equipment with prior
                               approval.
  Level 5 - Protocol Null:    Full neutralization protocols for catastrophic
                               threats.

APPLICATION GUIDELINES:
- Officers must attempt to apply the lowest necessary force to achieve
  compliance or safety.
- Escalation is permitted only if the subject's resistance or threat level
  increases.
- All use of force incidents must be reported within 2 hours with full
  documentation.

================================================================================
- Use clear, concise language when communicating on radio channels.
- Avoid unnecessary chatter to maintain channel clarity.
- Always identify yourself by callsign before speaking.
- Confirm receipt of commands with acknowledgment.
- Emergency traffic has priority; cease non-emergency transmissions when
  requested.
- Report any radio malfunctions immediately.
