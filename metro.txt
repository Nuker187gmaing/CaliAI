================================================================================
1. MISSION STATEMENT
================================================================================
The Metro Police Department keeps life awesome for everyone in our community:
residents, visitors, and businesses. The department believes in wellbeing and
celebrating the diverse folks we serve. Officers of MPD are all about doing
things the right way, with top-notch professionalism, integrity, and a strong
sense of responsibility.

The goal of the Metro Police Department is to ensure our community is safe,
welcoming, and inclusive. We're all about justice, protecting each person's
rights, and creating a feeling that makes the San Andreas metro area the best
place to be.

4. ACTIVATION (/duty) — ON DUTY REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

BREAKING CHARACTER:
- At NO point are members authorized to break character, even if someone is
  RDMing, VDMing, or breaking community guidelines.
- Members MUST NOT break character until an active Staff Member arrives on scene.
- Breaking character will result in disciplinary action per the Strike Policy.
- Refer to the CaliRP Global Departments SOP for further OOC/IC rules.

LOADOUT:
All firearms must be registered to the member in CAD. The member must also
qualify and possess all necessary firearm permits/licenses.
  1. Combat Pistol
  2. Pump Shotgun
  3. Taser
  4. NightStick
  5. Flashlight
  6. Carbine Rifle
  7. Less Lethal Shotgun

No other weapons permitted unless MK2 or Custom Weapons approved by Command or
server management. Such weapons must also be registered within CAD.

UNIFORM & PED POLICIES:
- Use any uniforms dictated for your rank by the department uniform roster.

VEHICLES:
- Use vehicles and modifications dictated for your rank or any rank below yours
  on the department vehicle roster.
- License plate format: ##MPD### — must be registered to the member's
  credentials within CAD.

IDENTIFICATION:
- You will be assigned a callsign and identified by it plus your CAD name
  while on-duty.
  Acceptable formats: 1N-01 | Andrew Z. / A. Zachary / Andrew Zachary
- Discord username while on-duty must match: First name + last initial OR
  first initial + full last name (e.g., Andrew Z. or A. Zachary).
- While on-duty, identify yourself as a law enforcement professional with your
  callsign and badge.
- If a Supervisor is requested, request one or have a DOHS Agent fill in.

RTO AND VOICE CHAT RULES:
- Bullying, segregation, racism, or any form of harassment will NOT be tolerated.
- Speaking about anything political is forbidden.
- NSFW content is prohibited and will result in a ban.
- Recruiting/advertising to members from other groups is highly prohibited.
- Toxicity and spamming will get you kicked/banned.
- Screaming in voice calls will get you muted.

BASIC RADIO ETIQUETTE:
- Mandatory language in public safety radio channels: English.
- Do not interrupt unless your traffic is pertinent to a serious emergency.
- Do not respond if you are not sure the call is for you; wait for your callsign.
- Never transmit sensitive, confidential, financial, or military information.
- Memorize callsigns and locations of persons you communicate with regularly.
- Avoid long, complicated sentences; divide long messages into shorter ones.
- Transmissions must be professional at all times (recorded and monitored).
- Keep communications clear, simple, and short. Use 10-codes as much as possible.
- Callsign and name must be displayed properly and match department credentials.

CAD:
- All officers must have the CaliRP CAD page open and logged in at all times
  while on duty.
- Status, callsign, and current call must be reflected in CAD in real time.

5. ON-SCENE PROTOCOL
================================================================================

PRIORITY SCENES:
- You are always permitted to respond to priority scenes.
- You may NEVER FORCE scene command. You MAY REQUEST to take scene command,
  especially on scenes where command isn't already established.

NON-PRIORITY SCENES:
- You are always permitted to respond to non-priority scenes (10-10, 10-11,
  10-17, 10-18, etc.).
- You are still NEVER allowed to force scene command.

6. DISCIPLINARY ACTION
================================================================================
Punishments are corrective actions due to failure to follow this SOP, Global
SOP, and/or CaliRP Policies.

AUTHORITY:
- High Command: may administer any punishment to anyone in the department.
- Low Command: may give written warnings, send off-duty, and suspend Supervisors
  and below.
- Supervisors: may give written warnings or send off-duty anyone below them.

STRIKE SYSTEM:
  Strike 1: No additional action unless otherwise dictated.
  Strike 2: 1-3 day suspension and promotion cooldown.
  Strike 3: Extended suspension and demotion OR termination.

ALTERNATIVE ACTIONS:
- Loss of certain duties, authority, or permissions.
- Administering a refresher training.
- Terminations are the sole discretion of High Command.
- If an offense warrants it, High Command may authorize a blacklist from the
  department.

CODE OF CONDUCT:
Members represent the Metro Police Department on and off duty.

Members are expected to:
- Respect all personnel in or outside the department.
- Perform assigned duties to the best of their abilities.
- Follow orders and respect chain of command.
- Maintain activity within the department and seek advancement opportunities.

Anyone who does not follow the Code of Conduct will receive disciplinary action
in conjunction with the Strike Policy.

7. PROMOTIONS & EVALUATIONS
================================================================================
Activity and professionalism determine your ability to promote. Minimum
requirements can be found in the department Discord.

RULES:
1. Promotion activity counted Sunday 12:00am EST to Saturday 11:59pm EST.
   You WILL NOT be given extra time past Saturday midnight EST.

2. Asking for a promotion = immediate strike.
   Breaking chain of command to ask for a promotion (e.g., contacting a
   Department Coordinator or Liaison) = strike + 2-week rank lock.

3. If you think you were missed for a promotion, open an Evaluation Ticket with
   your reasoning. This does not guarantee your promotion.

4. You must meet ALL promotion requirements including time in rank. No
   requirements = no promotion.

5. Double/triple promotions are at High Command's discretion. They are NOT
   guaranteed by hours alone. Asking for a double promotion = fastest way to get
   a strike.

6. If you have any active strikes or are on a one-strike policy, you are
   INELIGIBLE for promotion.

9. LEAVE OF ABSENCE, RESERVE, RESIGNATION
================================================================================

LEAVE OF ABSENCE (LOA):
- Submit an LOA request if absent for more than 1 week.
- LOAs exceeding 14 days must be approved by High Command.
- LOAs exceeding 30 days: High Command reserves the right to seek termination
  if no prior High Command approval for an extended LOA was given.
- LOA extensions must be approved by High Command.
- Once an LOA expires, officers may not take another LOA for 7 days.

RESERVES:
- Any officer may request Reserve status at any time.
- Minimum activity for Reserve status: 1 hour on-duty per week.
- Reserve Officers may NOT be part of or roleplay as any subdivision unless
  given direct explicit consent from High Command.
- Coming off Reserve: reinstated to original rank, UNLESS returning after 60+
  days — in that case, Time in Department resets and they are placed at Officer I
  (per GSOP).

RESIGNATION:
- Officers may resign at any time through a resignation ticket.

An officer may NOT resign to transfer if:
- They have come off LOA in the past 14 days.
- They have come off reserves in the past 60 days.
- They are Cadet or Probationary Officer, or joined the department in the past
  60 days.
- They have an active Strike, One Strike Policy, or Suspension.

REMOVAL FROM RANKS:
- High Command reserves the right to remove anyone from any rank or any "jobs"
  at any time regardless of department status, including but not limited to:
  TEU, SWAT, K9, ACU, MCD, FTD, IA, or any coordination jobs.

10. DOHS (DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY)
================================================================================
DOHS ensures adherence to CaliRP Global SOP. DOHS Agents may attach to a
member's call, conduct a traffic stop, or participate in general LEO roleplay.

- If questioned by a DOHS Agent, answer to the best of your ability and comply
  with any instructions given.
- Minor issues may be corrected at the scene; DOHS reserves the right to report
  findings to department High Command.
- For issues or concerns over DOHS conduct: use the CaliRP Department Ticket
  process or contact a DOHS Supervisor.
