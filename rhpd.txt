MISSION STATEMENT
================================================================================
The mission for the RockFord Hills Police Department is to protect with equality
and fairness, and to protect the public and the people of the RockFord Hills and
surrounding locations, to help when needed, wanted, and to interact and serve
the people within San Andreas. And to never leave another officer down or behind.

Signed by: C. Titan | Jordan M. | Dave S.

================================================================================
1.00 INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

1.01 - WELCOME
The RockFord Hills Police Department is tasked with keeping the citizens of San
Andreas safe and secure. We strive to help maintain the environment for those who
live and work in the state by enforcing its laws. This is made possible by the
dedication of our wonderful officers, supervisors, and command team each and
every day. The command team would like to thank you for choosing to be a part of
the RHPD.

1.02 - CORE VALUES
* Integrity — We hold ourselves accountable and demand the highest level of
  ethical and moral standards from all. We are role models acting with courage
  and building trust within the community to provide service and protection to
  the state of San Andreas.

================================================================================
2.00 CONDUCT
================================================================================

2.01 - UNITS CONDUCT
Units should always remember that they are a reflection upon the San Andreas
citizens. Units shall always stay professional and correctly deal with situations,
either by moral values, or by enforcing the law. Units should always be
professional and shall always exercise patience and discretion.

2.02 - DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS
There are multiple levels of disciplinary actions:

1. Verbal Warning — Wake-up call to fix whatever you were doing wrong.
2. Written Warning — Comes after a verbal warning; documented.
3. Strike System — Strikes given for major offenses or repeated mistakes.
4. Sent Off Duty — Not allowed back on patrol for a set time.
5. Retraining — Must go through classroom + field training / ride-alongs again.
6. Termination — Removed from department immediately; 2-week cooldown before
   joining any new department. May reapply after 2 weeks.
7. Blacklist — Removed from department and placed on the blacklist roster,
   possibly never allowed to rejoin RHPD.

All Cadets & Officer I's are on an automatic 1-strike policy until promoted.
This means any offense deemed worthy of a strike is subject to termination.

2.03 - ACTIVITY REQUIREMENTS
All units are required a minimum of 5 hours each period (week). Failure to meet
the minimum results in an inactivity strike. 1 inactivity strike is removed if
you are active for 2 periods (weeks). This does not apply to LOA.

To be eligible for promotion at the end of a period you must have 5 hours
patrolled and proper time in rank.

2.04 - PUNISHMENT GUIDELINES
Global punishment guidelines apply.

2.05 - PROMOTION POLICY
Promotions are granted when an officer reaches their rank's promotion guideline.
Weekly, Low Command meets with High Command and makes a list of everyone who
completed their requirements, then promotions occur at the department meeting.

Promotions are NEVER to be requested, asked for, or bribed for — this can result
in termination.

Promotion cooldowns: if an officer receives a double promotion, they are placed
on a 2-week promo cooldown.

2.06 - RTO ETIQUETTE AND 10-CODES

Radio Etiquette Rules:
* Mandatory language in radio channels: English.
* Do not interrupt unless your traffic is pertinent to a serious emergency.
* Do not respond if you are not sure the call is for you.
* Never transmit sensitive, confidential, financial, or military information.
* Memorize callsigns and locations you communicate with regularly.
* Avoid long sentences; divide long messages into shorter ones.
* Transmissions must be professional at all times (recorded and monitored).
* Keep communications clear, simple, and short. Use 10-codes as much as possible.
* Callsign and name must be displayed properly and match department credentials.

5.00 GENERAL PATROL
================================================================================

5.01 - TRAFFIC STOPS (10-11s)

Before the Stop:
* Observe a violation or have reasonable suspicion.
* Pick a safe spot to initiate (parking lots, highway breakdown zones, roadsides).
* Turn on lights and siren if needed.

Approach: Exit your vehicle, go around the back, approach from the passenger
side. Identify yourself and state the reason for the stop. Ask for license,
registration, and proof of insurance. Run information; return same way.

Positioning: Align right front headlight with the vehicle's back left brake
light, roughly 10 degrees to the left. Enter stop info in CAD (street name,
postal, nearest landmark). Call into RTO to advise of a 10-11.

After the Stop: Be courteous and professional. Determine proper action (verbal
warning, written warning, or citation). Return to car, turn off lights, call
10-8 into RTO.

5.02 - HIGH RISK TRAFFIC STOPS (CODE 5)
Performed when the registered owner has a warrant or vehicle was involved in
dangerous criminal activity.

Procedure:
1. Radio for a 10-32 (Code 3) and wait for backup before initiating Code 5.
2. Initiating unit positions behind suspect vehicle, driver's door open for cover.
3. Additional unit(s) position to the left at offset angles, doors open for cover.
4. All LEO stand behind vehicle doors with service weapons aimed at suspect vehicle.
5. Initiating unit (or highest rank) instructs driver/passengers.
6. Occupants exit one at a time, starting with driver; face away, walk back.
7. At least ONE unit must cover the vehicle at all times.
8. Once all suspects are in custody, units move and clear the vehicle.

5.03 - VEHICLE PURSUITS
Use normal department pursuit tactics and communication procedures. PIT
maneuvers and spike strips must be approved by a supervisor. DO NOT self-attach
to priorities if dispatch is in RTO.

5.04 - PERSONAL CHECKS
Units may perform property/business checks every 15 minutes when not responding
to a call.

5.05 - WHEN TO CALL A SUPERVISOR (10-71)
* A person requests to see your supervisor.
* You are unsure of what to do.
* A situation has escalated.
* It is a priority.
* An officer is injured.
* A unit is involved in a 10-50.
* Abnormal situations.
* Pulled over by another department's High Command or DHS/CIA/NGA.

5.06 - RESPONSE POLICY

Code 1 (Routine): Normal pace, no lights or sirens.
  Examples: Parking complaint, fender bender (no injuries), loud noise complaint,
  shoplifting, trespassing (no weapons), public intoxication (no danger), any
  call requiring only report writing, no one in danger.

Code 2 (Urgent): Lights; sirens only at intersections.
  Examples: Intoxicated driver report, domestic disturbance (verbal), road hazard,
  non-compliant subject (no warrants), any call requiring quicker response but
  no one in immediate danger.

Code 3 (Emergent): Lights and sirens; respond as quickly and safely as possible.
  Examples: Pursuits, 10-50 with injuries/fire/roadblock, life-threatening medical,
  domestic disturbance (physical), GTA, subject with warrants/combative, structure
  fire, bank alarm, homicide, kidnapping.

Special: Burglary in progress / hostage situations — respond Code 3, drop to
Code 1 when 5 blocks away.

5.07 - SCENE COMMAND POLICY
Highest-ranking supervisor on scene takes scene command ("Scene Command" in RTO).
If same rank, first supervisor on scene takes command. Incoming scene command
must be briefed by the outgoing unit. RTO must be informed of all priorities and
any shots fired or Code 4 status.

5.08 - MVA (MOTOR VEHICLE ACCIDENT) POLICY

If attached to a 10-50:
* Determine if injuries are present; Code 3 with injuries, Code 1 without.
* Notify fire to attach if injuries are present.
* On arrival: assess situation, determine if additional 10-32s needed.
* Call for a supervisor (dispatch may do this).

If involved in a 10-50:
* Incapacitated: Do NOT press panic button. Wait 10-20 minutes before messaging
  RTO for a 10-73 (Status Check). If a unit arrives first, RP your injuries.
* Not incapacitated: Inform RTO you were involved in a 10-50, request a 10-71,
  advise if anyone was injured.

5.09 - SELF ATTACHING POLICY
Command members may attach to any active call as supervisory unit. They are not
primary units and act only as supervisors unless a call is being mishandled.
Better judgment must be used; abuse results in disciplinary action and possible
rank reduction.

5.10 - USE OF FORCE POLICY
Units may use lethal force when they have a reasonable belief that the subject
poses an imminent danger of death or serious physical injury. A verbal warning
should be given before deadly force if feasible.

Firearm from inside vehicle — ALLOWED if:
* A suspect is actively shooting at you, other LEO, or civilians.
* A suspect is attempting to ram your vehicle and you cannot get out of the way.

Firearm from inside vehicle — NOT ALLOWED if:
* Going faster than 25 MPH.
* Taking a turn.
* Anyone other than the suspect is in the bullet's trajectory.

After discharging your firearm from inside a vehicle, stop the vehicle — it is
no longer road-worthy. Follow section 5.11.

Discharging at/into a vehicle: Only if you or someone else's life is in immediate
danger and no evasive action is possible.

5.11 - AFTER ACTION CLEARANCE POLICY
Whenever a unit discharges a firearm, they must request their immediate supervisor
to clear them from the scene. A unit cannot clear themselves. Supervisors must
determine if force was warranted based on:
* Severity of the crime.
* Whether the subject posed an immediate threat to life.
* Whether the subject actively resisted arrest.
Once the investigation is complete, the unit must provide the discharged firearm.

6.00 DEPARTMENTAL POLICIES
================================================================================

6.01 - LEAVE OF ABSENCE (LOA) POLICY
LOA requests must be approved by Low Command. While on LOA:
* Not allowed on duty as RHPD or in any RHPD-related activities.
* Not allowed on another FiveM server or active as a LEO (dual-clanning).
* Must list a valid reason (medical emergency, family emergency, exams, natural
  disaster, vacation, PC issues, no PC/internet access).
* Taking a break is NOT a valid reason.
* LOA must not be less than 7 days or exceed 14 days (Low Command approval).
* Long-Term LOA (15+ days): open a Low Command ticket; High Command must approve.
  May be asked to step down from a command position.
* After LOA ends: 7-day LOA cooldown before starting another LOA.

6.02 - TRANSFER POLICY
Transfers are available in the CaliRP Department Hub. Transfers come with a
60-day transfer cooldown in the new department (or the one you left).
Transfers can be denied for: punishment history, lack of activity, or being on
LOA (must be off LOA for 7 days before transferring).

6.03 - RADIO FREQUENCY
Must be in RHPD RTO (Discord) and the in-game radio frequency at all times while
on duty. In-game frequency: ###.## (see department Discord for current frequency).

6.04 - NAME STRUCTURE
Steam/FiveM name must include your callsign and name.
Format: RHP-01 | C.Titan
Must have a first and last name (order does not matter).

6.05 - LICENSE PLATE
Format: ##RHP### (replace # with any available number).
Set make/model to "Police Cruiser" and color to "RHPD Livery."
Incorrect plate format results in disciplinary action (starting with verbal/
written warning, escalating per 2.02).

6.06 - CAD SYSTEM
Must have a LEO character in the CAD system and be signed in while on patrol.
All required weapons and the vehicle plate must be registered in CAD.
Registrations must be updated each time you are promoted to a new rank.
Your LEO person may not have any outstanding tickets or arrests.

6.07 - ON DUTY POLICIES
* Must be in the correct RTO and in-game radio while on duty.
* 10-1 channels are for questions/help only — do not sit in them.
* 1-3 seconds between transmissions. No talking over or cutting off others.
* Correct badge, name, and in-game name must match and be displayed at all times.
* Headtag required at all times unless undercover.
* You may not staff your own incidents unless HA+ or select JHA+.

6.08 - SUBDIVISIONS

SWAT:
* Must be Officer III or higher.
* No current strikes or reports in the last 14 days.
* 7-day wait before reapplying if not accepted.

SPEED FREAKS (TEU — Pursuit & Off-Road Units):
* Must be Corporal or higher.
* No reports in the last 30 days.
* Pursuit Units: ramming when turning may result in removal from Speed Freaks
  and a 30-day strike.
* Off-road units must use trucks, SUVs, or lifted challenger/charger (SRT Pack).
  Stock F150, Explorer, Durango, Tahoe, etc. are too low for off-road use.
* 14-day wait before reapplying if not accepted.
