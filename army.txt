DEPARTMENT OF THE ARMY
UNIFORM CODE OF MILITARY JUSTICE (UCMJ)
Released: 06/16/2024 | Updated: 11/25/2025
================================================================================

ARMY CORE VALUES
================================================================================
- Loyalty:        Devote yourself to the Constitution, the Army, your unit, and
                  fellow Soldiers.
- Duty:           Fulfill your responsibilities and accomplish tasks as part of a
                  team, without taking shortcuts.
- Respect:        Treat others with dignity and respect while expecting others to
                  do the same. That's the Soldier's Code.
- Selfless Service: Commit to going a little further, enduring a little longer,
                  and adding to the effort in your own way.
- Honor:          Develop honorable habits and live up to our values in every
                  choice you make.
- Integrity:      Stand up for what's right, legally and morally, without doing
                  or saying anything that deceives others.
- Personal Courage: Face moral fear or adversity, while standing up for and acting
                  upon the things you know are honorable.

ARMY REGULATION 1001-01 | AVIATION
================================================================================
PURPOSE: Provides Army policy on aircraft operations, crew requirements, flight
rules, Army aviation procedures, aviation training, and aviation standardization.

AUTHORITY:
- S1: Command and unit staff are responsible for compliance and enforcement of
  aviation regulations.
- S2: The Military Police Corps oversees the administration and enforcement of
  aviation regulations. MP Corps may detain and arrest any defiant individual.

PERSONNEL AUTHORIZED TO FLY:
Army personnel assigned to the Army Aviation Branch above the rank of E-5
(any rank above Sergeant) are permitted to fly Army aircraft.

LOCAL FLYING RULES:
- All flights must comply with FAA regulations for the California region.
- Regardless of rank, all personnel must adhere to FAA rules.
- Any member of the CaliRP Department Coordination team may revoke your pilot's
  license at any time and for any reason (resulting in grounding).
- You must fly realistically while activated in an Army aircraft.

ARMY REGULATION 1002-01 | UNIFORMS & APPEARANCE
================================================================================
PURPOSE: Informs and disciplines Army personnel on how to properly utilize their
appearance, uniforms, accessories, insignias, and hair.

AUTHORITY:
- S1: Command and unit staff responsible for compliance and enforcement.
- S2: Military Police Corps and Quartermaster Corps oversee distribution and
  enforcement of uniform and appearance regulations. MP Corps may detain/arrest
  defiant individuals.

CUSTOM UNIFORMS:
- All custom uniforms must be sent for approval to your unit's headquarters.

APPEARANCE POLICIES:
- All accessories or additions to appearance must be related to the military genre.
- Male Hair: Must preserve a natural color. Males must keep a clean-shaved face
  on military installations. Berets allowed in deployments for Army Special
  Operations Command.
- Female Hair: Must not be in an unsecured ponytail, unbalanced, or lopsided style.
  Must preserve a natural color.

EYEWEAR:
- Sleek/trendy sunglasses with initials or exaggerated fashions are PROHIBITED.
- Eyewear is not to be worn with ASUs; Eyepatches are allowed.
- Sunglasses must be black or dark gray framed.
- PROHIBITED at Fort Zancudo unless given specific authorization by Army Staff:
  Balaclavas, bandanas, gas masks, aviator sunglasses, monocles.

STANDARDIZED UNIFORMS:
[ACU] Army Combat Uniform:
  - Worn on military establishments.
  - Army Patrol Cap must be worn with the ACU, with the cap's corresponding rank.
  - Rank tabs must be worn with the ACU and Army Patrol Cap.
  - Berets can replace the Army Patrol Cap if requirements for your respective beret
    are met within your division.

[APFU] Army Physical Fitness Uniform:
  - Worn on military establishments during physical training.
  - PT belts must be worn with PT clothing.
  - Summer: No headgear allowed; PT shirts and PT shorts to be worn.
  - Winter: PT windbreaker and PT trousers to be worn, plus a black bandana.

[ASU] Army Service Uniform:
  - Official formal uniform for inspections, ceremonies, briefings, or graduations.
  - PROHIBITED when engaged in combat, law enforcement, physical training, or
    strenuous/laborious work.

Class B's:
  - Only used for classroom events (ASU is worn for more formal events).
  - PROHIBITED when engaged in combat, law enforcement, physical training, or
    strenuous/laborious work.

[ICVC] Improved Combat Vehicle Coverall:
  - Worn by operators and pilots of tanks and helicopters, ONLY when in operation
    of those vehicles. If not in operation, wear ACU.
  - Exception: Personnel in the 1st Cavalry Division may wear ICVC as they please.
  - Must be accompanied by the corresponding helmet/accessories for your division.

Berets:
  - May be worn by those who have passed their entrance program for their respective
    division and have been placed into a brigade/battalion.
  - Only wear the beret pertaining to your unit, nothing else.

Campaign Cover:
  - May ONLY be worn by the Training and Doctrine Command.
  - Only worn with: Army Combat Uniform or Army Service Uniform.
  - Positions pertaining to a Drill Sergeant may wear the cover.

DISCHARGES AND CONDITIONS
================================================================================
PURPOSE: Ensures all service members are discharged correctly (honorable, general,
or dishonorable) and outlines blacklist conditions.

DISCHARGE CLASSIFICATIONS:

HONORABLE DISCHARGE - May be issued if:
- Member requests discharge AND:
  - Has served 7+ days total (commissioned officers: 7+ additional days post-commission)
  - Has no recent history of misdemeanor or misbehavior in the past 7 days
  - Is recognized for distinguished performance
  - Has received approval from their Commanding Officer

GENERAL DISCHARGE - May be issued if:
- Member requests discharge AND:
  - Has served 7+ days total (commissioned officers: 7+ additional days post-commission)
  - Has held little to no history of misdemeanor or conduct in the past 7 days
  - Has failed to meet expectations and ideal standards
- Member requested an extended leave of absence
- Member is failing to meet proper standards
- Member violated a minor regulation requiring discharge despite warning
- NOTE: A member may NOT request Reserve status to avoid termination, demotion,
  or disciplinary strikes. Must have fully completed required hours for both the
  previous month and current month to be eligible for Reserve status.

DISHONORABLE DISCHARGE - May be issued if:
- Member has committed moderate, major, or capital crimes under the UCMJ.
- Any other reason deemed necessary by Command Staff.

ARMY WIDE BLACKLIST - May be issued if:
- Member committed major abuse of privileges causing large impact on installation
  or command.
- Member committed an act categorized as a security concern or other misconduct.
- Any other reasons deemed necessary by Army Staff.

DISCHARGE OUTCOMES:
Honorable Discharge:
  - Commissioned Officers returning: demoted to O-1 (Second Lieutenant).
    Misconduct reported to Army Staff = immediate decommission.
  - Sergeant Majors [E-9A through E-9C] returning: demoted to E-7 (Sergeant First
    Class). All other NCOs returning: demoted to E-2 (Private Second Class).
  - Above is nullified if transferring to another branch.

General Discharge:
  - Commissioned Officers returning: demoted to E-7 (Sergeant First Class).
  - Noncommissioned Officers returning: demoted to E-2 (Private Second Class).
  - Above is nullified if transferring to another branch.

Dishonorable Discharge:
  - Regardless of commission status, all individuals returning: demoted to E-1 (Private).
  - Individuals may not attend Officer Candidate School or receive a commission
    for 14 days.

Blacklist:
  - Army-wide blacklist = removed from department permanently.
  - Cannot return to any factor of the Army unless properly appealed to Army
    Headquarters of California Roleplay Department Coordination.

TRAINING AND RECRUITMENT PROCESS
================================================================================
PURPOSE: Establishes the process by which a citizen may enlist in the US Army.

PHASE ONE:
- Submit an application reviewed by the Recruiting and Retention College for
  acceptance/denial into Basic Combat Training (BCT).
- Alternatively, participate in an interview at an open recruitment hosted by a
  member of the Recruiting and Retention College.

PHASE TWO:
- After Phase One, a Private in BCT must successfully complete classroom and field
  training under a certified Drill Instructor before moving to Phase Three.

PHASE THREE:
- After all phases are complete, Private is promoted to E-2 (Private) in their
  chosen division.
- All training must take place with a member at or above the rank of Sergeant.

COMMON ACCESS CARD (CAC) POLICIES
================================================================================
PURPOSE: Establishes policies and procedures for the Common Access Card and
access to Fort Zancudo.

AUTHORITY:
- S1: Command and unit staff responsible for compliance and enforcement.
- S2: Military Police Corps oversees CAC policies. MP Corps may detain/arrest
  defiant individuals.

PERMITTED DEPARTMENTS:
- Departments permitted inside Fort Zancudo are on the Department of Defense
  Contractor's List.
- "Stationed" status: access to stage for operations, takeoff in aircraft, or use
  war room for briefings.
- "Base Patrol" or "Outer Base Security" status: access to patrol and maintain
  base security.
- CIA and DHS: access only for aircraft takeoff, except CIA SAD team and DHS
  agents with a base access card.

BASE ACCESS CARDS:
- CaliRP Department Coordinators are granted access at all times when on official
  business.
- DHS Fort Zancudo Base Access Card holders are also granted access.
- See Department of Defense SOP Section 4.0 for more information.

COMMON ACCESS CARDS:
- Regardless of rank, you MUST provide your Common Access Card to any Military
  Police officer via /headtag or the /showbadge command.

GATE DUTY:
1. Approach the vehicle or individual attempting to enter Fort Zancudo.
2. Ask what their intentions are on Fort Zancudo.
3. If they identify as military: ask for their CAC.
4. If they don't identify as military: ask why they need access and check with
   a base commander (O5+) or the contractor's list.
5. Upon denial: ensure they leave Fort Zancudo's surroundings.
6. If permitted to enter: perform a full search of the individual and vehicle.
   (See Department of Defense SOP Section 2.8 for more details.)

ARMY REGULATION 1100-01 | DEPARTMENT STRUCTURE
================================================================================
STRUCTURE:
- Headquarters (HQ): Highest ranking officers; all Corps report to and take orders
  from HQ.
- Corps: Largest unit made up of multiple divisions. Commanded by a Brigadier General.
- Division: Independent unit with its own purpose. Commanded by any Field Grade
  Officer (usually O-6 / Colonel).
- Division Command: Takes orders from their commander. Must be at least 2nd Lieutenant;
  cannot be higher than Colonel. Highest rank in Division Command is Executive Officer
  (second in command of the division).

NOTES:
- Divisions are run by their Commanding Officer, NOT by the Corps Commanding Officer.
- Division Commanding Officers have autonomy to run their division within this structure.
- Divisions are NOT permitted to lower the rank requirement for "command" or adjust
  the structure above; they may add ranks below it.

================================================================================

LEAVE OF ABSENCE (LOA) POLICY | AR 1.1-1
================================================================================
PURPOSE: Allows approved Army members to be temporarily exempt from activity
requirements for the duration of their approved leave.

ELIGIBILITY & APPROVALS:
- Any member at the rank of Private or higher may request an LOA.
- LOA requests may be approved by Low Command, Trial High Command, or High Command.
- Standard LOA may NOT exceed 30 days unless extended with written HC approval.

ACTIVITY RESTRICTIONS WHILE ON LOA:
- Members on LOA are NOT permitted to be in-game under any circumstance.
- Exception: if the member is actively performing staff duties.
- If found in-game under another department or playing outside of staffing while
  on LOA, disciplinary action will be taken by the enforcing supervisor.

COOLDOWN & FREQUENCY:
- Once an LOA expires, member must wait a minimum of 30 days before submitting
  a new LOA request.

LOW COMMAND RESPONSIBILITIES DURING LOA:
1. Review and approve/deny LOA requests in a reasonable timeframe.
2. Ensure all LOA entries are properly recorded on the roster.
3. Monitor members to ensure LOAs are not overstayed or misused.
4. Forward extension requests to High Command for review and approval.

END OF LOA & ACTIVITY REQUIREMENTS:
- LOA officially ends on the date approved by Low or High Command.
- If an extension is denied, the LOA ends on the originally approved date.
- After returning from LOA, members are responsible for meeting all required
  activity hours. Failure may result in warnings, demotion, or termination.

RESTRICTIONS ON REQUESTING AN LOA:
- Members on Activity Warning may NOT request an LOA until they return to Active.
- Members Under Investigation (UI) may NOT request an LOA. After investigation
  concludes and UI status is removed, an LOA may be requested only if absolutely
  necessary.

PROMOTION RESTRICTIONS:
- Members may NOT receive promotions while on an LOA.
- IRL military or HC-exempted members are only excused from department hours,
  not MOSs or subdivisions (not applicable before 12/03/2025).
