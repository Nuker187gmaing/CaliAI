CALIFORNIA ROLEPLAY - ARMED FORCES
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES
Last Updated: 05/24/2026 | Authors: Leo D., J. Rice, S. Flye, J. Mills
Signed By: Department Coordination Team & Ownership
================================================================================

All Armed Forces Regulations and Standard Operating Procedures are to be followed,
respected, and read over by every member of the Armed Forces regardless of rank.

By agreeing to this document you acknowledge that reprimands could come from
violations of any rules/regulations listed. If you feel this document is incomplete
or unfair, reach out to the Armed Forces Command Team or Department Coordination Team.

================================================================================
1.0 | AGREEMENT AND BASICS
================================================================================
All members are aware that if violations are a concern to their Command Team they
could be investigated and removed. All points are to be considered with full
discretion — these rules apply to ALL ranks within the Armed Forces.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 | WEAPONIZED VEHICLES AND ROSTER
- You may use any weaponized vehicle on your department vehicle roster that is
  allowed based on your department's rules.
- Any usage of weaponized vehicles must directly relate and correlate to an RP
  scenario for your respective department.
- Improper usage of weaponized vehicles will result in a strike within the Armed
  Forces.
- Refer to the Global Department SOP and your own current department SOP as needed.
- Usage of vehicles with a gunner seat: a body (member) MUST be in the gunner seat.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.2 | VEHICLE SPEEDS
- Speed on all land vehicles: 45 MPH speed limit inside Fort Zancudo.
- Outside Fort Zancudo: follow all posted speed limits.
- Tanks, LAVs, and similar vehicles shall NOT leave the base unless given correct
  approval.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.3 | LEO AND ARMED FORCES ROLEPLAY
- Armed Forces may be requested for large Cartel/Militia presence or dealing with
  weaponized vehicles, etc.
- Armed Forces and LEO/Federal departments may ONLY roleplay together with
  authorization from an Office of the Secretary of Defense member, DHS 400
  callsign, or Main Dept LC.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.4 | RTO AND UNICOM
- While on as your military department, you MUST be in the Armed Forces UNICOM RTO
  in the MAIN DISCORD.
- UNICOM should be strict at all times. Failure to comply will lead to a UNICOM
  strike.
- Exception: trainings (respective training rooms only).
- Any operations within your department must be conducted within respective Armed
  Forces Operations Channel(s).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.5 | FLYING AND FAA
- While flying, you MUST be in radio 149 (FZ Aviation Radio) while in FZ airspace
  for takeoffs, landings, taxiing, etc.
- NO aircraft within Armed Forces shall fly below ATC and Radio Tower.
- Exception: OSD Approval may allow flying below ATC and Radio Tower within FZ
  airspace.
- You may fly below ONLY when taking off or landing your jet or helicopter.
- You may NOT take off from a taxiway at any time.
- Abuse of aircraft could result in removal from your department.
  (Civilians on the ground should not see your plane.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.6 | HEAD TAGS AND DUTY BLIPS
- You MUST wear your department's head tag and duty blips while active on duty or
  roleplaying as your military department at all times.
- Head tags should NEVER be abused.
- Any department-related tags must be approved by VCJCoS before being locked to
  your department.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.7 | ROLEPLAYING OFF DUTY OR AS CIVILIAN
- ANY individual NOT in a Military Department or the Armed Forces is NEVER to be
  allowed in any military vehicle or Armed Forces UNICOM/RAID channels.
- Off-duty Armed Forces members may NOT use their Armed Forces CAD character while
  on as a civilian and do "Off Duty RP," nor attempt to come on base as "Off Duty
  Armed Forces."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.8 | TANK USAGE OFF BASE
- Tanks or any other tracked vehicle shall NOT be driven off Fort Zancudo unless
  there is a direct military operation APPROVED by OSD or NG Command to contain
  a hostile threat.
- Tanks should always be shipped on a trailer when needing transport.
- Having someone in the tank or heavy-tracked vehicle while on a trailer is NOT
  allowed.
- Lock all transport vehicles on or off trailers including the semi driving.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.9 | IFF (IDENTIFICATION FRIEND OR FOE)
- Every department MUST offer IFF training inside their main training course or
  boot camp.
- Cadets must be taught how to identify themselves and others.
- You MUST always identify yourself within RTO/UNICOM while taking off, landing,
  and taxiing.
- Before opening fire on an unidentified aircraft, you MUST attempt to contact
  them first.
  * Green Zone: must be intercepted.
  * Red Zone: may be shot down.
- Anyone flying over restricted airspace not in UNICOM, not in FAA Radio (150.0)
  in-game, and not responding must be eliminated.
- Any civilian aircraft must be ESCORTED off the airspace.
- ONLY E-5+ MAY FLY.

2.0 | UNIFORMS & VEHICLE STRUCTURE
================================================================================
Any Armed Forces unit active on duty MUST be in the proper uniform per their
department's uniform guidelines at ALL times.

UNIFORM RULES:
- On base: wear ACUs.
- On patrol or convoy: wear combat uniforms.
- While using a jet or helicopter: wear an appropriate flight uniform.
- Indoors: remove your cover/hat unless actively being raided or at a ceremony.
  This includes anything on top of your head and in your ears.
- When transferring from a JSOC to normal base operation: put on your OCPs.
- Only American flags are permitted on uniforms (no British flags, etc.).
- Midget peds are PROHIBITED when on as Armed Forces (passive RP only peds).

VEHICLE RULES:
- Follow your department's vehicle guidelines and rank structure.
- Vehicles must be realistic — NO sport cars, bikes, or supercars on base.
- Vehicles must have plates in accordance with GSOP.
- No vehicle may have a window tint darker than limo unless operating as JSOC.
- Vehicles may NOT have both visor lights and a lightbar at the same time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 | USE OF ARMED FORCES DEPARTMENT PERSONALS
- No member of Armed Forces, regardless of rank or server status, may drive a
  military vehicle not allowed for their department and rank unless authorized
  by the OSD.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2 | ARMED FORCES PERSONAL STRIKE POLICY
No member of the Armed Forces, regardless of rank, is exempt from breaking Armed
Forces rules.

DEPARTMENT STRIKE SYSTEM (per department):
  Strike 1: Warning
  Strike 2: Suspension
  Strike 3: Removal

ARMED FORCES GENERAL STRIKE SYSTEM (2 strikeout policy):
  Strike 1: 30-day suspension
  Strike 2: Permanent Blacklist

NOTE: Armed Forces OSD/DC are exempt from following this.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3 | LEO AND DHS PULLING YOU OVER
- If an Armed Forces vehicle is pulled over by any blue/red lights, they MUST pull
  over and comply.
- LEOs may pull over Armed Forces vehicles and call DHS for further interactions.
- If no DHS is available, provide your information and they will let you go.
- If a DHS agent pulls up beside your vehicle and asks you to pull over, you MUST
  pull over IMMEDIATELY.
- Listen, respect, and remain calm when DHS is handling you. Follow all orders they
  give and handle the situation after returning to base.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.4 | NICKNAMES AND IDENTIFICATIONS
- Members MUST have their pay grade, rank, and name displayed with their badge
  while in Fort Zancudo RTO, Joint Operations channels, and on duty.
- Do NOT use unrealistic callsigns (e.g., "Thug 1-1" or "Lebron 1-1").
- Callsigns must be realistic and professional (e.g., "Reaper 1-1", "Dagger 1-1").
- Example formats:
  O-10 | Gen. | John D.    /    E-1 | PVT | J. Doe    /    O-3 | CPT | John Doe

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.5 | PROPER ROLES AND PERMISSIONS
- Request your department role in the role-request channel in the Armed Forces Discord.
- Members are only allowed in ONE Main Armed Forces department and ONE Sub Armed
  Forces Department at a time.
- Ensure you have the Whitelisted DoD Role in the main discord.
- If you are E-5+, ensure you have the Whitelisted DoD Supervisor Role in the main
  discord.
- Having another Armed Forces Department's role in the Armed Forces Discord is NOT
  allowed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.6 | RTO AND UNICOM ETIQUETTE (2 STRIKE POLICY)
- REQUIRED to maintain professionalism and strictness in RTO channels at all times.
- NO general conversations held in RTO — only Armed Forces related information.
- No talking over others or arguing in RTO. THIS WILL BE STRICTLY ENFORCED.
- If rules in UNICOM are not followed, a Commanding Officer will issue a warning.
- 2 warnings = UNICOM Strike.
- 2 UNICOM Strikes = Department Strike.
- All Armed Forces Personnel should NOT be in Department offices or management
  offices inside Discord Canary while clocked on as Armed Forces Personnel.
  Exception: if recruiting, you may be in your respective recruitment VC instead
  of UNICOM.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.7 | ARRESTING, MEDICAL, AND JAILING DEAD/WOUNDED INDIVIDUALS
If anyone is killed by Armed Forces on base:
1. Approach the individual and first put in: /me checks pulse
2. If dead: /me calls coroner (or CC), then use M menu to send to coroner.
3. If alive after checking vitals: apply medical assistance or radio for someone
   who can; then hospitalize, question, and/or jail/arrest them.

CORONER TIMER RULES:
- Player raids the base and KILLS 1+ Armed Forces members: coroner for 300 seconds.
- Player raids the base and does NOT kill any Armed Forces members: coroner for 150
  seconds.
- AT NO TIME should these features be used to skip respawn timers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.8 | GATE SECURITY AND PROPER GATE PROCEDURES
ALL members MUST follow these procedures for individuals approaching/entering base:

Step 1: Approach the vehicle or individual attempting to enter Fort Zancudo.
Step 2: Ask what their intentions are on base.
Step 3: If they identify as a Military Branch, ask for their CAC.
Step 4: If they don't identify as Military, ask why they need access and CHECK
        WITH A BASE COMMANDER (O-5+ or highest ranking member on duty) if they
        should be allowed in.
Step 5: Upon denial, ensure they leave the base's surroundings.
Step 6: If permitted entry, perform a FULL search of the individual and vehicle.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.9 | ARMED FORCES DEPARTMENT MOVEMENTS

DEPARTMENT TRANSFERS:
- May happen at any time.
- If you leave or are removed without transferring: 14-day cooldown before joining
  another Armed Forces department.
- To transfer: go to Department Hub Discord and initiate a transfer request.
- Upon transfer: mandatory demotion of 1 rank (can be more if receiving HC wishes).

DEPARTMENT SHUTDOWN TRANSFERS:
- When an Armed Forces Department shuts down: 72 hours to transfer to another
  Armed Forces Department.
- Below O-3: lateral transfer.
- HC or LC: automatically become O-3 unless approved by LDC+ to go higher.
  This is case-by-case; NO exceptions.

SUBDIVISION TRANSFERS:
- Transferred to the equivalent subdivision they were previously in.
- Must still go through training and pass the new department's training.
  NO exceptions.

3.0 | RAIDING FORT ZANCUDO
================================================================================
Raiding Fort Zancudo without well-developed roleplay beforehand is considered
FailRP & RDM and against server rules.

- Putting in a /GME saying "Raiding FZ" or "Raiding FZ in 5 minutes" is NOT
  considered well-developed roleplay.
- No OOC talking about defending Zancudo and encouraging others to raid.
- All raids must remain within RP at all times.
- If Armed Forces choose to end the scene, staff may be called and the scene
  disregarded.
- Armed Forces members who go off duty must wait 30 minutes before raiding
  the base (powergaming rule).
- All vehicles, weapons, and peds must remain realistic throughout RP.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 | CAYO PERICO RULES
- Raiding must only be done when properly scouted (foreign land, not part of map).
- Driving/flying around or "relaxing" at Cayo is NOT allowed.
- Simply going there to "go there" or mass kill is NOT allowed.
  (Scouting and gathering intel is fine — scouts must be discreet.)
- If found at Cayo, they CAN and WILL shoot on sight with no questions asked.
- Tanks to Cayo ONLY by trucking, boating, or airlifting — NOT driving on roadways.
  Driving tanks on roadways to get to Cayo may result in punishment. NON-NEGOTIABLE.
- ONLY Operators of the Joint Special Operations Command (JSOC) may conduct
  operations on Cayo.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 | OFF-BASE ROLEPLAY
- Militarized vehicles off base only allowed during RP (transport convoy or called
  upon by state authorities — see 1.3).
- E-5+ required to host and launch a convoy off base.
- Must make a pre-planned route and communicate in radio 146.00.
- Only MARKED vehicles are allowed outside of Fort Zancudo.
- JSOC Operators permitted off base for Intel/Operations missions.
- When responding to high-priority scenes: MUST match force or be one level above
  the current threat.
  Example: Suspects have rifles → bring a 50.CAL.
           Suspects have 50.CAL → bring a bigger 50.CAL or use a 40MM.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 | LEO LIGHTS ON ARMED FORCES VEHICLES
- Military vehicles with LEO lights shall NOT use emergency lights off base except:
  * To head back to base Code 2 or 3 if the base is under attack.
  * If you are active Military Police.
- If stopped by DOHS or NG and asked for proof the base is being attacked, comply
  respectfully.
- Amber/Yellow lights MAY be used during convoy operations to escort convoys off
  base.
- All rules regarding the Emergency Services SOP must be followed.
- If on as MP: you must have an MP Livery on your vehicle.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 | LOCKDOWN PROCEDURES AND ESTABLISHING KOS

ALPHA:
- Base on complete lockdown.
- All non-combat personnel must be inside the bunker for safety.
- Base under attack.
- Kill on sight on all hostile actions.
- DOHS, CIAA, NCIS & VGA are allowed on base during Code Alpha.

BRAVO:
- Base on shutdown.
- All personnel must be in combat vehicles.
- Contractors & federal units NOT allowed entry.
- DOHS, CIAA, NCIS & VGA are allowed on base during Code Bravo.

CHARLIE:
- Heightened awareness.
- All contractors & federal units must be escorted by MPs.

DELTA:
- Normal Base Operations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5 | MILITARY POLICE
- MPs may pull over other departments (besides NG) inside base for valid reasons
  (e.g., not following speed limits, driving on taxiways/runways without reason).
- Must be in proper uniform, using the Military Police Gangtag, and in radio 148.00.
- You are NOT allowed to put charges on an Armed Forces member's CAD character.
- If an Armed Forces member commits a felony on base: refer it with evidence (clips)
  to their Department Command.
- If you pull over a contractor: forward it to an OSD member with evidence.
- AT NO TIME will jail exceed 800 seconds per server rules.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.6 | ROEs AND USE OF FORCE INSIDE OF BASE
- Gate-guard/MP: check CAC cards or verify the contractor's list.

RULES OF ENGAGEMENT:
- If they speed through the gate and KILL any gate-guard/MP: immediately classified
  as KOS — you are permitted to fire on them.
- If they speed through the gate and run WITHOUT killing anyone: try to detain/catch;
  you are NOT permitted to shoot them unless they are holding a weapon (bat, pistol,
  rifle) or are in an armored vehicle.
- Always CALL OUT YOUR TARGETS to prevent friendly fire.
- Civilian contractors on base: immediately locate them and get them to safety
  (armored vehicle or inside barracks).
- AT NO POINT use explosive rounds on a soft target — this applies even to tier 5
  terrorists. DO NOT use tanks against ground troops.

RESTRICTED AREAS / KOS ZONES:
- Fort Zancudo ATC Tower: KOS if inside, outside trying to break in, or on top.
- Fort Zancudo Radio Tower: KOS if on the Radio Tower.
- Fort Zancudo Medical Bay & HQ: KOS if breaking into Medical Bay or HQ.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.7 | CLOSE AIR SUPPORT (CAS)
- CAS on a soft target must ONLY be approved by OSD or NG Command during an
  operation outside of base.
- Do NOT strike Fort Zancudo Military Base with explosives or non-explosive
  ammunition unless approved by OSD.
- CAS ONLY provided on armored, bulletproof, or weaponized vehicles.
- A pilot reserves the right to deny an air strike if deemed unsafe, likely to
  cause casualties, or conditions aren't right.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.8 | JSOC AND OPERATIONS OUTSIDE OF BASE
- Operating outside Fort Zancudo: ONLY done by Special Operations groups approved
  by the respective Team Leader or Office of the SOD.
- Special Operations groups MUST be in an Armed Forces Ops Channel while conducting
  operations.
- All RP rules must be followed; violations result in punishments regardless of rank.
- Operating outside Fort Zancudo is a PRIVILEGE — treat it as such.
- If contacted by a Department Coordinator requesting you return to base, obey
  immediately and wait for further instructions.
- A raid format MUST be filled out when raiding any property on or off US soil.
- Vehicle restrictions still apply; maintain the highest degree of realism.

JSOC BEARD POLICY:
- JSOC members ARE permitted to grow and utilize a beard while deployed.
- Upon return to base: member MUST change to a ped without a beard.
- JSOC members are NOT permitted to remain active on base after conclusion of their
  raid; must change immediately after the after-action report.
- Dress Blues policy ALWAYS supersedes this — beards must be removed immediately
  when wearing dress blues regardless of recent return from JSOC.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.9 | LEO REINFORCEMENTS AND QRF (QUICK REACTION FORCE)
- Highest in UNICOM will respond to any Main Dept Low Command+ LEO requesting
  Armed Forces assistance.
- Highest in UNICOM will pick 3-6 people depending on the severity of the situation.
- IF THERE ARE NOT A MINIMUM OF 3 OTHER PEOPLE IN UNICOM BESIDES THE TEAM LEADER,
  YOU ARE NOT PERMITTED TO ACTIVATE AS QRF.
- Highest in UNICOM is the team leader — what they say goes.
- Uniform is determined by your department HC.
- QRF is LIMITED to ONLY the DOD_Humvee1 or DOD_Humvee2 while activated as QRF.
- PROHIBITED QRF weapons: Explosive weapons and Throwable Explosives.
- PERMITTED QRF weapons: Flashbangs and Breach Charges.
- QRF ONLY responds to stationary scenes — vehicle chases are NOT permitted and
  must be left to LEO.

4.0 | ALLOWING DEPARTMENTS ON BASE AND DOHS BASE ACCESS CARD
================================================================================
- "Stationed" status: allows said department/subdivision to stage for operations,
  take off in helicopters/planes, and use the war room for briefings.
- "Base Patrol" or "Outer Base Security" status: access to patrol and maintain
  base security.
- CIAA and DHS: access ONLY for taking off in helicopters/planes.
  Exemptions require OSD member approval.
- DHS Members with a DHS Base Access Card may patrol within the base and enter
  Armed Forces UNICOM to assist in day-to-day operations.
- Department Coordinators: granted access at all times when on official business.
- Any reports on abuse of power from the above must be reported to Armed Forces DCs
  immediately.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 | MILOOPS/UNIVERSAL/PERSONAL VEHICLE USAGE
- Access to a vehicle does NOT mean you can use it whenever.
- Must follow Armed Forces guidelines — e.g., if activated as a department that
  wouldn't realistically have a tank, you may NOT use a tank.
- NATO Vehicles are allowed.
- You are NOT allowed vehicles that are non-American (e.g., cannot use a SU-47).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 | WEAPON POLICY AND USAGE
- Front sling: always permitted.
- Back sling: only for heavy weapons (grenade launcher, sniper rifles, LMGs).
- Do NOT run around base slinging RPGs unless actively using them.
- Suppressors: ONLY permitted for operations; must be used on primary rifle.
  All snipers MUST include a suppressor.
- RESTRICTED WEAPONS: AP Pistol, Ghost Weapons, Dragon's Breath Rounds, Incendiary
  Rounds.
- Mini Gun usage: must be approved by an OSD member.
- Breaching charges (C4) and small explosives (grenade/flashbang): ONLY for
  breaching rooms and clearing hostiles in enclosed buildings.
- Extended mags, regular mags, and drum mags are allowed.
- RPG POLICY: Only permitted on bulletproof vehicles, aerial machinery, heavy
  ground artillery, and targets who are holding explosives or have recently used
  explosives.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 | S.A.M SITE RP
- S.A.M/Anti-Air Defence Site RP is permitted on base.
- MUST be stationed in ONE position; NOT allowed to move for the entirety of
  operating a S.A.M.
- To stop: exit the vehicle and DV (delete) the S.A.M before continuing other RP.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.4 | CAC POLICY
- You MUST present your CAC to any MP who requests it on base.
- This includes all base entries, MP traffic stops, and other situations requiring
  a CAC.
- Use /showid or the God's Eye menu to present your CAC.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.5 | DRONE USAGE
- Drones do NOT fall under the plane category.
- Allowed altitude: 350-450ft maximum.
- Drone should NOT operate BELOW these requirements (don't let the enemy see you
  easily).
- Strictly for RECONNAISSANCE purposes against an OpFor.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.6 | LA OPERATIONS
- All Combat Ready troops within Armed Forces are allowed to station in LA and
  combat local Cartel/Militia crimes.
- STRICT regulations:
  * Only drive Blacked-out Chargers or Tahoes.
  * Must wear uniform codes 19022 or 19023 at ALL TIMES.
  * Only engage designated groups; do NOT interfere with or take over any LEO scene.
  * May only assist LEO if specifically requested.
  * NOT permitted to partake in: Bank Robberies, Drug Busts, Hostage Situations,
    10-99, or 10-80.
  * If you encounter any of the above, notify local LEO and do NOT interfere.
  * You may NOT exceed into Mexico or Blaine County at ANY time.
  * Must utilize an Armed Forces Operations or Joint Operations Channel while
    conducting combat missions in LA.
  * MUST notify Base Commander+ before conducting combat operations.
  * If FZ requires your assistance: R.T.B. (Return to Base) IMMEDIATELY.
  * Required to have 4 or more people when operating off base.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.7 | SEARCH & RESCUE OPERATIONS
- Armed Forces members may conduct Search & Rescue on civilians on land or at sea
  under STRICT RP.
- S&R members MUST be properly trained before activation.
- Land vehicles for S&R missions are NOT permitted; must use Helicopters or
  Watercraft.
- May conduct rescue operations all over the state (including Roxwood/Paleto).
- To launch a S&R mission: must be in the presence of an E-5+ and Base Commander
  must sign off on the mission.
- After rescue: fly or transport to the nearest medical center and return to base.
- Uniform must be Combat Medic styled during S&R missions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.8 | MORTAR USAGE
- Members who have purchased "Mortars" are permitted to use them on duty.
- Mortars are NOT bound to the "Hard Target, Soft Target" rule — may be used on
  people or vehicles.
- Must be used realistically.
- Must be accompanied by a TWO-MAN TEAM: one Spotter and one Mortar Fire Technician.
- You may NOT station your mortar within 150 meters of your target.
- You may NOT bomb the base using mortars unless approved by Base Commander+ and
  the section must be closed off in RP for repairs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.9 | HARD TARGET / SOFT TARGET
A HARD TARGET is: anyone using explosives (RPG, sticky bombs, etc.), anyone in a
bulletproof or bombproof vehicle, anyone in tanks, weaponized helis, jets, or
using a 50.CAL on base.

RULES:
- At NO time may you bomb people with explosive cannons.
- At NO time may you bomb people with explosives (exemption: Mortar).
- You MAY ONLY use explosive cannons on armored vehicles or buildings.
- You MAY ONLY use bombs on armored vehicles or buildings.
- You MAY ONLY use bombs/explosive cannons on people who are ACTIVELY using
  explosives.

- 1 Personnel should ALWAYS be in a Manned Humvee 50.CAL covering the main gates

  MINUTES.
- ALL personnel must have their CAC reviewed and verified before entering the base.
- All Civilian Contractors must be verified fully on the contractor list and have
  their CAD checked (SSN verification) before entry.
- All Department Contractors must be verified at the gate and only be on base for
  their assigned contract.
- All contractors entering base must be notified to and approved by the current
  Base Commander.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 | BASE COMMANDER
- The Base Commander ensures good order and discipline on Fort Zancudo.
- Anyone of a lesser grade MUST follow all lawful orders of the Base Commander.
- The highest-ranking Armed Forces member on duty automatically becomes Base
  Commander.
- Must debrief the new Base Commander if someone higher rank comes on duty.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.3 | GROOMING STANDARDS
All members MUST be clean shaven. NOT PERMITTED:
- Silly faces (huge nose, inverted faces).
- Beard while on duty (exception: see 3.8 JSOC rules).
- Mustache extending past the corners of the mouth.

Senior-ranked members may inform offending members to shave; failure to do so
may result in disciplinary action.

MUST be clean shaven when wearing dress uniforms including:
- Service Alphas (full dress jackets).
- Service Bravos (shirts bearing medal ribbons and rank on shoulders).

HAIR STANDARDS:
- Hair must be short and neatly cropped.
- Must not extend past the halfway point of the ear.
- No more than 2 inches in length.
- Must not stick out under any headdress (berets, peaked caps, tactical helmets).
- Dreadlocks and afros must be kept short and neatly cropped.
- Hair should not poke out from inside headdress.

5.4 | PMC RULES AND REGULATIONS

CONTRACTING PROCEDURES:
- May be contracted by civilians or law enforcement.
- CANNOT be involved in any criminal activity or contracted by gangs/criminal
  enterprises.
- Must follow all laws at all times.
- Must uphold GSOP and Armed Forces SOP.

RULES AND REGULATIONS:
- When activated as a PMC, you are expected to follow all rules in the Armed Forces
  SOP.
- Permitted to patrol in an Armed Forces Operations Channel or Joint Operations for
  contracting missions.
- For Civilian Contracting Missions: communicate with them via a radio frequency.

RAIDING GANGS AND CRIME FAMILIES:
- PMC must still follow JSOC Guidelines and Armed Forces SOP raiding rules.
- Must be contracted out to raid gangs/families by Civs or Armed Forces/LEO only.
- A PMC CANNOT raid FZ, Marine, or be contracted by another gang or crime family.
- PMCs CANNOT be contracted to conduct any illegal/unlawful activities.

PMC ROLEPLAY:
- PMC may roleplay with other civs and Armed Forces on and off base.
- Civs are NOT allowed on base unless on the Armed Forces Contractors List.
- PMCs may NOT partake in Corrupt Roleplay.
- PMCs may not violate any laws; all contracts must be FULLY LAWFUL.

OPERATIONS OUTSIDE OF BASE:
- PMCs are allowed to conduct raids on gangs and crime families following all Armed
  Forces SOP and JSOC SOP rules.
- NO PMC member may call in CAS off base while conducting contracts for civilians.
  PMC may call in air support only while with other Armed Forces or on department
  operations.
- PMCs are permitted to raid in Mexico.

ACTIVATIONS:
- PMCs may activate as Air Units, Marine Units, and tactical units.
- May use weaponized helicopters within reason.
- When supporting LEOs as a conventional air unit: use NON-weaponized aircraft
  (e.g., C-130, MH6).
- When activating as an aerial unit: MUST be in radio 149 and communicate with Fort
  Zancudo ATC Tower at all times.
- May use weaponized boats (patrol boat and 50.CAL dinghy).
- NOT permitted to use weaponized vehicles when supporting LEOs as a conventional
  marine unit.

ARMORED VEHICLES USAGE OFF BASE:
- PMCs may use 50.CAL trucks/vehicles off base ONLY in non-populated areas.
- PROHIBITED areas: Sandy, Grapeseed, Roxwood, Paleto, Inner LA.
- Must be used realistically — not just for patrolling in 50.CALs.
- All 50.CAL gunners MUST be able to be shot when out.
- Tanks and APCs must be transported legitimately and used far from populated areas
  in pre-coordinated RP.

PATROLLING:
- When not contracted, MUST be on base and help perform basic base duties (gate
  duty, etc.).
- Only permitted to patrol off base when contracted out and completing contracting
  missions.

5.5 | ARMED FORCES COMMAND STRUCTURE
Structure: 5 HC spots, 2 THC spots, up to 6 LC spots. Departments will NOT exceed
their HC, THC, and LC spots.

HC:   O-10, O-9, O-8, O-7, O-7
THC:  O-7, O-7
LC:   O-6 (x6)

Only Main Armed Forces Departments and PMC can clock in.
O-7 HC+ can clock in as staff. They CANNOT staff their own staff scenes.

5.6 | CUSTOMS AND COURTESIES
All members must follow Customs and Courtesies Guidelines. Supervisors and command
are responsible for maintaining standards. Violations may result in verbal warnings,
written warnings, suspension, or removal.
(Armed Forces DC and LEO DC are NOT exempt.)

SALUTING:
- Salutes or verbal acknowledgments ("Sir/Ma'am") will be rendered to ranks of
  Lieutenant and above (O-1+) during formal situations (ceremonies, inspections,
  promotions).

MILITARY ETIQUETTE:
- Treat all personnel, regardless of rank or department, with respect at all times.

RADIO ETIQUETTE:
- Use proper callsigns and unit numbers at all times.
- Speak clearly, professionally, and only when necessary.
- Begin transmissions with your unit ID followed by the recipient's ID
  (e.g., "Dagger 1-1.")
- Avoid slang and excessive radio chatter.

All Armed Forces members must follow all rules regarding Fort Nova base rules.
Failure will lead to Written Warning, Suspension from Fort Nova, or removal from
your department.

- OSD withholds permissions to handle any unit on the base including NCIS.
- Everyone must maintain strict mannerisms as on Fort Zancudo, following all Armed
  Forces rules and regulations.

NCIS FZ RULES:

- NCIS does NOT have permission to conduct Torture RP on Fort Zancudo.
- NCIS must follow Armed Forces SOP when activating on Fort Nova.

ARMED FORCES RULES ON FORT NOVA:
- NCIS THC are allowed to send off Armed Forces members on Fort Nova ONLY.
- Armed Forces members can fly and drive tanks at Fort Nova.

5.8 | HC MEMBERS IN OTHER ARMED FORCES DISCORDS
- Armed Forces Command Team members DO NOT NEED TO BE in other Armed Forces Discords.
- To join another Armed Forces Discord: ask the Acting O-10 of that department.
- If removed from that Discord: the HC member must reach out with a valid reason.
- To get a member banned from a specific department Discord: must get VCJCoS (OSD)
  approval plus proof/valid reason before the ban.
- Exception: if the member is breaking Discord TOS, account is compromised, or
  sending explicit images/messages — they can be banned right away WITH proof.

5.9 | ARMED FORCES RESERVES GUIDELINES
- A member in the Reserves Program will NOT get a rank higher than O-1.
- When in Reserves: NOT assigned to any JSOC team; however, can still be assigned
  to MP, Armed Forces Fire, or the Air Division.
- Upon coming off the Reserves Program: placed at a rank the command team feels is
  fit.
- NOTE: Command Team cannot instate higher than O-3 unless approved by LDC+.
