OVERDRIVE | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES
================================================================================

SECTION 1.0 | INTRODUCTION
================================================================================
Overdrive is a passive car club business designed to bring together car enthusiasts
for organized events within the city.

Members will have the opportunity to drive unique and custom vehicles provided by
the business founders. In addition to casual cruising, we host street takeover events,
where we temporarily close off roads to showcase our vehicles, perform controlled
drifts, and create an exciting, immersive experience.

We may also engage in street racing scenarios, along with police pursuits, to enhance
the thrill of the experience.

Overdrive aims to be a dynamic hub for car enthusiasts to connect, share their passion
for vehicles, and participate in immersive automotive roleplay.

We also include mechanic roleplay elements to foster deeper interaction with the
community and add realism to the club's activities.

SECTION 3.0 | ROLEPLAY
================================================================================
When active as Overdrive, expect to do some car cruises, car meets, drift meets,
street racing, and some street takeovers.

We also provide the opportunity to do mechanic role-play. All mechanical roleplay
must be strictly passive. This means no shortcuts, unrealistic behavior, or breaking
immersion.

We take pride in our driving abilities, custom and unique car designs, and roleplay
capabilities. Ensure that you are properly following all Business, Verified Civilian,
as well as CaliRP community rules while on duty.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3.1 | LOCATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We have a wide variety of locations to enhance your role-play opportunities, ranging
from mechanic shops to work on our vehicles, parking lots and garages for car meets,
and even locations for street and circuit racing.

A list of all Overdrive locations can be found in the Overdrive Discord.

When using one of our listed locations, you MUST use the appropriate premade GMEs.
GMEs can be located within the "Locations-and-gmes" channel within our Discord,
and also in Section 5.0 of this SOP.

SECTION 4.0 | RULES & ROLEPLAY GUIDELINES
================================================================================
While On Duty, you are required to follow all server and business rules.

WEAPONS POLICY:
- You are NOT to use weapons under ANY circumstances, as we are a passive business.
- Exception ONLY: Handpicked members who have received the "Gun certification" role.
  This may ONLY be used for self-defense if another civilian poses an immediate threat
  to your life and you are unable to de-escalate. ABSOLUTE LAST RESORT.
- You may NOT use ANY weapon against non-civilian personnel.
  This includes LEO / DOD / EMS / FD / Peace Officers.
- You may NOT discharge your firearm at LEO/Peace Officers.
  Doing so will result in IMMEDIATE termination and possible BLACKLISTING from Overdrive.

LEO PURSUIT GME REQUIREMENT:
When initiating a chase with an LEO/Peace Officer, you MUST make the following GME:
  /gme ^*~r~Overdrive ~w~member is planning on fleeing from police while being pulled over

SECTION 4.0 EXTENDED | ADDITIONAL RULES & ROLEPLAY GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Always listen to your superiors and follow their instructions.
- Ensure you are already inside the RTO BEFORE doing /job to go on duty. Never
  leave the in-game radio while clocked in.
- While on duty, your name within the Business Discord MUST have your Overdrive
  Callsign. Your in-game radio name must match accordingly for identification purposes.
- Do NOT spawn in vehicles or repair them in the middle of the road. This is
  unrealistic and a display of poor roleplay.
- Although we are a car club that does races, ALWAYS drive realistically.
- Do NOT speed unrealistically, ramp off hills, or engage in GTA Driving.
  All of these are prohibited by our guidelines.

Failure to abide by all Department, Business, and Server Rules will result in
disciplinary action.

SECTION 4.1 | UNIFORM, VEHICLE, & NAME GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UNIFORMS:
- Overdrive does not have a uniform requirement (exception: on-duty mechanic).
- You are prohibited from wearing outfits that poorly reflect on the group.
  This includes troll outfits or anything against CaliRP guidelines/server rules.

VEHICLES:
- You may drive any vehicle so long as it reflects the current roleplay the group
  is engaged in.
  Example: If we are doing street racing, do NOT pull up in an offroad truck.

NAME FORMAT:
Once accepted into Overdrive, your name in the Overdrive Discord will be set as:
  OD-XXX | Name

It is YOUR responsibility to copy this format to your displayed name in the
Businesses Discord.

RTO PREFIX BY RANK (ONLY to be used by the listed rank):
  ! - High Command
  # - Low Command
  $ - Supervisor

SECTION 7.2 | CORRUPT MECHANIC
================================================================================
Overdrive members holding the rank of No Hesi+ are allowed to do this type of RP.

How it works:
- Go to a random mechanic shop that is NOT one we mainly use.
- Post /gme to get clients.
- Strip a good part from the client's vehicle and replace it with the same part
  but less durable (all actions posted in a /gme).
- If the client starts questioning you, make up excuses (e.g., "it's a new part,"
  "I saw it needed replacing," etc.).
- The stolen parts are to be deep cleaned in RP and placed in storage to be used
  for our non-corrupt mechanic shop.

If done correctly, there should be no issues with cops or clients.
- If police are called: try to reason with them and diffuse the situation.
  Do NOT run on foot; you must surrender unless you can flee in a vehicle.
- If a client metagames: clip it, and after the scene report the user to staff.
