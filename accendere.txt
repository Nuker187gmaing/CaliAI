OFFICIAL OPERATIONS MANUAL
================================================================================

SECTION 1 | INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

1.1 BACKGROUND
Accendere Corporation began as a group of friends who met up for a motorcycle ride.
These friends were always mischievous and liked to cause mayhem throughout their
neighborhood. One day, one friend suggested they roll around as a gang, but without
the usual gang issues. They started stealing and selling black market items for quick
money, but not everyone agreed.

The leader suggested they dress up in bright colors and call themselves the "Light
Riders," but that name didn't stick. After research, they settled on a name with
roughly the same meaning in Latin, as they were practically starting a business.
This militia was the start of something amazing.

Over time, some members left, and new ones were sought. "Business" grew rapidly.
The group managed to sell drugs, advanced tech, and much more — transforming their
smaller operation into a Corporation. They grew closer as a family, almost always
traveling in convoy style to avoid aggressive groups. They always made a statement,
no matter how small or large the scene.

The moral of this group was to create something for wealth and impact. They became
known as Accendere Corp. — a militia that entered Los Santos for a new beginning,
and has since taken over.

================================================================================
SECTION 2 | GENERAL INFORMATION
================================================================================

2.1 WHAT WE DO
Accendere Corporation is a militia that conducts services for the most elite
contracts. Accendere Corporation engages in private security, bounties, drug sales,
and more. Accendere Corporation is known for their brutality and stealth while
leaving their targets unidentifiable.
