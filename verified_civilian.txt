WHAT IS VERIFIED CIVILIAN
================================================================================
Verified Civilian is a department dedicated to creating immersive and realistic
roleplay. Being a Verified Civilian means striving to improve and elevate roleplay
experiences and enjoyment for everyone in the community, including ourselves.
Whether you want to roleplay as a criminal, a hardworking citizen, or a clown,
the roleplay possibilities truly are endless.

When you fully commit and immerse yourself in roleplay, not only will your
experience be more enjoyable, but you'll also contribute to the enjoyment of
those around you. We encourage you to let your imagination run wild and create
characters that truly stand out with unique personalities, styles, voices, and
habits.

Upon becoming a Verified Civilian, you'll gain access to a range of rank perks
detailed in the #rank-perks channel, with more perks and benefits unlocking as
you rank up, allowing greater opportunity to explore more diverse roleplay
scenarios.

CHAIN OF COMMAND
================================================================================

OVERSEER (00):
  Oversees department and provides support to High Command.

HIGH COMMAND (01-10):
  Head of Verified Civilian. Overlooks and manages the entire department, guiding
  Verified Civilians and Command team. Has final say on decisions.

LEAD MANAGER (11-15):
  Overlooks and assists Managers with daily duties and hosts major events.

MANAGER (16-25):
  Overlooks and assists Lead Supervisors with daily duties, processes applications,
  roster maintenance, and hosts major events.

LEAD SUPERVISOR (26-35):
  Overlooks and assists Supervisors with daily duties, hosts interviews and exams,
  moderates chat, and hosts pop-up events.

SUPERVISOR (36-60):
  Hosts interviews and exams, moderates chat, and hosts pop-up events.
  This is the FIRST RANK to approach with questions or concerns.

IMPORTANT: Please follow the Chain of Command at all times and work your way up.
DO NOT GO STRAIGHT TO HIGH COMMAND.

CODE OF CONDUCT
================================================================================
- Follow CaliRP community rules, whether in Discord or in-game.
- Provide realistic and detailed roleplay at all times.
- Do NOT disrespect, bully, or harass anybody within Verified Civilian or in the
  CaliRP community.
- When utilizing Verified Civilian rank perks, you MUST wear your Verified
  Civilian Headtag.
- When told by LEO to leave a scene you did not initiate, you MUST leave.
- You must NOT participate in roleplay found offensive, inappropriate, considered
  NSFW, or against CaliRP rules.
- You should remain in character, even if you have been RDM'd. You must wait for
  staff to arrive before going OOC (out of character).
- Hold yourself to the highest standard while representing Verified Civilian.
- Upper Class Civilians+ are allowed to steal LEO vehicles (7 minutes prior
  roleplay required).
- ONLY Elite Class Civilians are allowed to participate in fake LEO RP.
- You MUST remain passive while working at a Verified Business and wearing the
  corresponding Verified Business headtag.
- If asked for your callsign, you must give it (may be for a praise or a report).
- If you leave the Verified Civilian Discord for any reason, High Command has full
  discretion over the rank you will be reinstated at.

Failure to adhere to the rules will result in appropriate disciplinary action.

ROLEPLAY SCENARIO GUIDELINES
================================================================================

VALUE OF LIFE:
The importance of cherishing your life cannot be overstated. When faced with a
life-threatening situation (e.g., someone wielding a weapon), the priority should
be to protect yourself and seek ways to de-escalate. If an individual has a
firearm pointed at you, attempting to reach for anything would likely result in
deadly consequences.

FEAR FOR FREEDOM:
The primary goal of Verified Civilian is to promote immersive passive roleplay.
Priority scenarios are allowed, however we encourage civilians to engage in
situations where their character prioritizes their freedom. Unless your character
has active warrants in the CAD, there is generally no justification for fleeing
from a routine traffic stop.

GTA DRIVING:
Avoid unrealistic driving like speeding over 150 mph or careening off cliffs.
If participating in drag or street racing scenarios, ensure they remain realistic
and consider the legal and safety consequences. Extreme speeding endangers lives
and can lead to serious charges like reckless driving or felony speeding.

NEW LIFE RULE:
If your character experiences a "death" within roleplay, you must adhere to the
New Life Rule, requiring you to forget all events that transpired within the last
15 minutes to prevent engaging in Revenge RP.

The above rules are some of the most important, in addition to other basic roleplay
rules like VDM, RDM, and FRP.

HOW TO SHOW ACTIVITY AND EARN A PROMOTION
================================================================================
1. CLOCK HOURS: Roleplaying while wearing your Verified Civilian headtag is
   critical to be considered for a promotion. The more you roleplay as a Verified
   Civilian, the more your dedication will stand out.

2. POST PHOTOS: Post photos in the photos channel. This highlights your
   participation in Civilian roleplay and activity within the server. Photos must
   be captured yourself. Any photo works!

3. ENGAGE IN CIVILIAN-GENERAL: Verified Civilian is not just a department — it's
   a community. Participate in civilian-general to contribute to conversations,
   share ideas, meet new people, or simply say hello. Join VCs, create RP
   scenarios, and form connections.

4. ATTEND VERIFIED CIVILIAN EVENTS: Every week, Event Coordinators organize events
   announced in the #events channel. These events are often overseen by Command
   and outstanding roleplay could lead to a promotion.

5. EXCEPTIONAL ROLEPLAY: Actively roleplaying in-game with your Verified Civilian
   head tag on while providing exceptional roleplay could get you noticed by a
   Command member, resulting in a field promotion on the spot.

IMPORTANT: Being active in just one area or just clocking hours alone does NOT
guarantee or grant a promotion. Demonstrate dedication across multiple areas.
Failure to maintain some level of activity could result in a demotion. You are
excused from activity if you are on an approved LOA. LOAs may NOT exceed 1 month
unless approved by High Command.

================================================================================
Activity is expected across all ranks. However, Middle Class, Upper Class, and
Elite Class Civilians MUST actively roleplay with the Verified Civilian headtag
on in order to remain at that rank.

Hours are automatically logged when you put on and take off your Verified Civilian
headtag.

USEFUL COMMANDS (in the bot commands channel):
- /evaluate  - Provides a summary of your activity status in relation to
               promotional guidelines.
- /myhours   - Provides total server and Verified Civilian duty hours.

PROMOTIONS
================================================================================
Promotions are held BI-WEEKLY on Sundays.

- Do NOT ask or open tickets about missed promotions unless instructed by a
  Command member.
- Asking for a promotion or evaluation can result in a STRIKE or DEMOTION.
- Promotions are based on quality of roleplay and activity.
- Showing activity in ALL areas gives the best chances of being promoted.
- Consistent activity across multiple areas could result in promotions every two
  weeks up to the rank of Upper Class.
- Middle Class and Upper Class ranks are very hard to achieve.
- Elite Class ranks are LIMITED and reserved only for those who have demonstrated
  the highest quality of roleplay, are actively engaged in the community in a
  positive way, and have consistent activity over an extended period of time.

DISCIPLINARY GUIDELINES
================================================================================
Disciplinary action depends on the severity of the offense. The Internal Affairs
team will review reports and evidence without bias and determine appropriate
punishment.

EXAMPLES:
- FRP: Could result in a warning on your record.
- Continued problems: Strike or demotion.
- Mass RDM or VDM: 30-day strike, demotion, or termination from the department
  for 14 or 30 days (requiring reapplication).
- Repeated offenses: Punishments grow in severity.
- Blatant disrespect towards Community or Command members: Multi-rank demotion,
  removal from Verified Civ, or blacklisting.

ALL punishment decisions made by the Command team are FINAL and must be respected.
Arguing about punishment decisions may lead to further disciplinary action.

================================================================================
RESIGNATION
================================================================================
PROPER RESIGNATION: Open a resignation ticket found in the ticket selection.
This allows the potential for reinstatement at your prior rank, depending on
time away.

IMPROPER RESIGNATION: If you leave the Discord without resigning, you will NOT
be reinstated and will be required to reapply.
