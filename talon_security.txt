PURPOSE AND SCOPE
================================================================================
These Standard Operating Procedures make up the essential framework governing
all operations carried out by Talon Security personnel. They are not suggestions
but obligatory protocols that ensure our agents remain professional and on the
right side of the law.

- Preservation of Collective Safety and Well-being: These protocols are made to
  reduce risks to our staff, clients, and the general public.
- Maintenance of Unwavering Professionalism: These guidelines establish a
  consistent, elevated standard of demeanor, communication, and action.
- Effective and Coordinated Execution of Duties: The SOPs guarantee that all
  security responsibilities, from routine surveillance patrols to crisis
  response, are performed with precision, uniformity, and maximal efficiency.
- Assurance of Legal and Ethical Compliance: All procedures are in line with
  state law and regulatory requirements, safeguarding the company and its
  employees against potential liability.

================================================================================
I. GENERAL CONDUCT & PROFESSIONALISM
================================================================================
- Respect and Professionalism: Always treat clients, fellow agents, and the
  public with respect. Maintain a professional attitude and appearance on duty.
- Integrity: Uphold the highest standards of honesty and integrity. Do not
  engage in illegal activities or misuse company resources.
- Confidentiality: Do not disclose any information on a client or operation to
  unauthorized individuals.
- Reporting: Report any incidents, issues, or suspicions to your supervisor and
  hold everyone accountable.
- No Bullying or Harassment: ZERO tolerance policy for bullying or harassment of
  ANY kind. If you or someone within the team is experiencing this, secure
  evidence and report it.
- Clocking In: Absolutely NO clocking in by yourself if you do not meet the rank
  of Security Agent.

================================================================================
II. EQUIPMENT & UNIFORMS
================================================================================

UNIFORMS:
- Wear the uniforms assigned to your rank at all times.
- Lead Operatives and above are allowed custom uniforms that must be approved by
  a member of the command team via the #uniform-approval Discord channel.
- Uniforms must be suitable for the assigned job (no casual clothing at a bank
  job).

AUTHORIZED WEAPONS:
- Lethal: ARs, SMGs, and pistols.
- Non-lethal: Bean Bag shotguns, tasers, and batons.

PROHIBITED WEAPONS:
- No military-style weapons, no MGs, RPGs, or general explosives.
- No suppressors or drum magazines (they are illegal).

WEAPON HANDLING:
- Handle all firearms responsibly and in accordance with server rules and
  realistic gun safety practices.
- All guns are to be treated as if they are loaded.
- Do not point a weapon at anyone unless justified.

CITIZENS ARREST:
- As a security business, you can place people under citizens arrest using
  zipties.
- You are NOT to take the person away from the job site; call the police and
  wait for them to arrive.

VEHICLES:
- Authorized: Only use Talon Security vehicles when on duty.
- Do NOT register the plate - it is registered to the company.
- Plate must be TSTALON and the vehicle must always be black.
- Follow all traffic laws while on duty unless safety is compromised.
- Do NOT go over 140 MPH.
- Prohibited: Do not abuse the BP Escalade or the CTS. No ramming whatsoever
  with the Escalade. Breaking these rules is an AUTOMATIC TERMINATION.
- Civilian vehicles are limited to emergency situations only; must have little to
  no damage and have all 4 doors/wheels.
- Pursuit Policy: We do NOT engage in pursuits.

================================================================================
III. OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES
================================================================================
- Client Security: The primary objective is the safety and security of our
  clients and their assets. Prioritize de-escalation over confrontation
  whenever possible.
- Communication (RTO): When on duty as Talon, you MUST remain in RTO at all
  times unless a command member needs to speak with you in their office.

================================================================================
IV. RULES OF ENGAGEMENT (ROE)
================================================================================
- De-escalation First: Lethal force should be a last resort. Always attempt to
  de-escalate situations verbally before resorting to force. Immediately turning
  to violence is a HUGE mistake. Always prioritize the safety of all parties
  involved. Refer to the Use Of Force Continuum.
- Self-Defense: Personnel are authorized to use necessary force to defend
  themselves or their clients from imminent harm.
- Defense of Property: Force may be used to protect client property from active
  theft or damage, but always prioritize life over property.
- Reporting Use of Force: Any instance of force used must be thoroughly reported
  to a supervisor as soon as safely possible.

  Junior Operative              TS-67 to TS-92
  Security Agent                TS-93 to TS-166

PROBATIONARY AGENTS:
  Trial Agent                   TA-100 to TA-167

COC PROTOCOL:
- Follow orders from higher-ups. If you have concerns, address them through the
  appropriate chain of command.
- Respect the COC. Start at the bottom; if they cannot help, it will be handed
  up higher.
- If you need to question someone in command, send a well-thought-out reason
  with evidence (if possible) to anyone in High Command.

VI. MISCELLANEOUS REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

HOURLY REQUIREMENTS:
- Lead Operative (Supervisors) and above: minimum 6 hours per week.
- Senior Operatives and below: minimum 3 hours per week.
- If you cannot make the minimum, contact Command to be placed on Restricted
  Hours.

CALLSIGNS:
- You will be issued a Trial Agent callsign (TA-XX) at the end of training.
- After completing 3 jobs as Talon Security, you will be promoted to Security
  Agent (TS-XX).
- Wear your callsign and badge in RTO and in the Talon Security Discord.

PROMOTIONS:
- Do NOT ask any command members for promotions.
- You will earn it through your logs, attendance, attitude, and leadership.

SUBDIVISIONS:
Must be Junior Operative or higher to apply (exceptions possible via ticket):

  RTD    - Recruitment and Training Division
  K9     - Canine Division (handlers and dogs for searching and tracking)
  Vanguard - Top-Tier drivers (Handpicked)
  N.E.S.T. - Neutralization Espionage and Surveillance Team (undercover agents)
  S.O.A.R. - Specialized Overwatch And Aerial Reconnaissance (air division)
